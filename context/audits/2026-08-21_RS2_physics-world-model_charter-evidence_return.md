# RS-2 — Physics/World-Model Evidence for the Charter (filed 2026-08-17)

## §0 Executive summary (≤1 page; the against-evidence stated at strength)

**The headline, in one sentence: the general-purpose "physics/world-model layer for the home" does not exist as a shipped thing anywhere — what actually ships and demonstrably makes money is NARROW, SINGLE-HAZARD physics inference with an accountable buyer — and the literature's model side is real but almost entirely pre-deployment, which means the seed's physics-aware direction is neither validated nor refuted by the field: the field simply has not arrived where the seed points.**

Five findings carry the decision weight:

1. **The frontier "world model" wave has not touched the home.** V-JEPA 2 (Meta, 2025-06-11: 1.2 B params, 1 M+ hours of video, robot pick-and-place 65–80 %) names no home application; Genie 3 (DeepMind, 2025-08) is interactive environment generation; Cosmos 3 (NVIDIA, 2026-05-31, "the world's first fully open omnimodel… with physics accuracy") targets robotics, AVs, and industrial "smart spaces" — the word "home" does not appear [VERIFIED-WEB, §1.1.5]. The one funded vector aimed at the home is sensor-side: Archetype AI's Newton model partnered onto Infineon 60 GHz radar with "TVs, smart speakers and smart home appliances" named as targets (2023-10-24) [VERIFIED-WEB, §1.2.6]. The category the seed would enter is EMPTY of incumbents and empty of consumer vocabulary — "world model" and "digital twin" appear nowhere in consumer smart-home marketing on this lane's searches [VERIFIED-WEB by absence, §1.4.1], consistent with the L-E return's independent absence finding for "physics-aware" (its §4.1).

2. **What ships is narrow physics with a payer.** The three commercial proof points: Ting (electrical-arc signatures; 1 M+ homes protected; State Farm expanded free deployment toward 2 M sensors, 2024-04-02; "saved nearly 10,000 homes" claimed) [VERIFIED-WEB]; Phyn ("high-definition pressure wave analysis," ultrasonic flow, learn-then-act) [VERIFIED-WEB]; and the thermostat thermal model (Nest's shipped learning of "how quickly your home heats and cools, how efficient your system is, and the weather" — the most-deployed home physics model on earth) [VERIFIED-WEB]. Each is one physical channel, one hazard/benefit, inference LABELED as prediction or alert, and — decisive — an economic principal who internalizes the value (insurer, utility, energy bill). Nobody ships, or even markets, a general home-state world model (§1.2).

3. **The model literature is real, deployment-thin, and cheap-at-inference / costly-at-trust.** The strongest field review (Khabbazi et al., Applied Energy 399, 2025) finds 104 MPC/RL field demonstrations, judges 71 % of protocols unreliable, and prices the reliable remainder at 16 % (residential) / 13 % (commercial) weighted-average cost savings — with only 13 of 104 papers reporting deployment cost at all [VERIFIED-WEB]. Physics-consistent architectures (PCNN: up to 40 % better than RC on 3-day horizons, physically consistent BY DESIGN) exist and matter, and the umbrella reviews call the field "nascent" in so many words [VERIFIED-WEB, §1.1.1]. The counterweight is RCT-grade: Brandon et al. (NBER w30482) find "little evidence that smart thermostats have a statistically or economically significant effect on energy use" across 18 months and 16 M+ hourly readings — because HUMANS override the model [VERIFIED-WEB, §1.1.6]. The lesson for the charter is not "models don't work"; it is that in homes, the binding constraint on model value is TRUST AND INTERACTION, not accuracy — which is the exact surface the substrate's honesty law already owns [INFERRED, so labeled].

4. **The ambient-sensing land-grab is the market's actual "world-model" move, and it is running now.** Hue MotionAware (Zigbee RF sensing on 3–4 lights per area; Bridge Pro exclusive; alerts behind €0.99/mo) shipped 2025; Xfinity WiFi Motion is live on XB7+ gateways ("not a home security service"); Samsung announced ambient sensing + generative Map View (2025-01-22, rolling out "throughout 2025 and 2026"); Aqara FP2 mmWave does 30-zone spatial positioning locally [all VERIFIED-WEB, §1.2.4–1.2.5]. Presence — the lowest-hanging home inference — is being commoditized into radios and priced at €1/month, while its privacy semantics stay unclaimed (the Substrate Thesis §7-Q9 territory). Value implication per the thesis's own §2.7: the sensing primitive commoditizes; the HONEST-INTERPRETATION layer above it is where value lands and nobody is standing there.

5. **The economics of Pi-class inference bound what an in-home model layer can be.** Verified current numbers: the AI HAT+ 2 (2026-01-15, $130, Hailo-10H, 40 TOPS INT4, 8 GB) targets 1–1.5 B-parameter models [VERIFIED-WEB]; deep-NILM at Jetson-class misses real-time (2.83–3.68 s inference per 0.83 s window) [VERIFIED-WEB]; while RC/gray-box thermal models, Kalman-class fusion, and Bayesian occupancy run effectively free (a 2017 system ran MPC with an ON-BOARD EnergyPlus on a Raspberry Pi 3 [VERIFIED-WEB]). The hardware draws a bright line: PHYSICS-STRUCTURED SMALL MODELS are at home on our reference hardware; FRONTIER-CLASS world models are not, and remain cloud-bound — which is the substrate bet at the silicon layer, re-confirmed on 2026 prices (§1.3; consistent with the L-E return §4.2).

**Decision shape (full statement §3):** (A) ADOPT-NOW-SCOPED exists and is genuinely narrow — a bench-side thermal/physics characterization slice riding the already-adopted P1-A corpus, prediction-labeled surfaces deferred to v1.x; (B) DECLINE-WITH-TRIPWIRES is fully respectable on this evidence and the tripwires are nameable and dated; (C) RE-COMMISSION-DEEPER has five specific questions this lane could not answer, four of them answerable only at the repo or by talking to buyers. PM-REC at §3.4, one line, non-binding. Coverage honesty at §4.

### §1.1 Literature — world models / physics-informed models applied to buildings and homes

#### §1.1.1 Thermal dynamics: the mature core, and it is genuinely deployable

- **Gray-box RC identification is the durable foundation.** The canonical method paper — Bacher & Madsen, "Identifying suitable models for the heat dynamics of buildings," *Energy and Buildings* (2011) (full text: http://henrikmadsen.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/Journal_article_-_2011_-_Identifying_suitable_models_for_the_heat_dynamics_of_buildings.pdf) [REPORTED] — established stochastic RC model selection from operational data, and the lineage now extends to open corpora of calibrated residential models ("Grey-Brick Buildings," an open dataset of calibrated RC models of Dutch residential heat dynamics, https://www.researchgate.net/publication/367395240 [REPORTED; fetch blocked 429 this lane]). Load-bearing property for us: identification runs offline on ordinary telemetry (temperature, setpoint, runtime, outdoor temperature) — exactly the shape of data an event log accumulates for free.
- **Physics-consistent neural architectures fix the failure mode that matters.** Di Natale, Svetozarevic, Heer & Jones, "Physically Consistent Neural Networks for building thermal modeling: theory and analysis," *Applied Energy* (2022); arXiv:2112.03212 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.03212) [VERIFIED-WEB]: PCNNs are "physically consistent — by design and even on unseen data" (formally proven) while beating a classical RC model by "up to 40 %… on 3-day long prediction horizons." Companion result: Gokhale et al., "Physics informed neural networks for control oriented thermal modeling of buildings," *Applied Energy* (2022); arXiv:2111.12066 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.12066) [VERIFIED-WEB] — PINN variants are "data-efficient, requiring less training data" than unconstrained networks. Both were developed against real-building operational data (the Swiss NEST living-lab lineage; see §1.1.4). Read for the charter: the literature's own direction of travel is TOWARD physics-constrained smallness — structure substituting for data and compute — which is the only model class that fits §1.3's hardware reality [INFERRED from the cited results].
- **The umbrella reviews say "nascent" in their own voice.** Dong et al., "Physics-informed machine learning for building performance simulation — a review of a nascent field," *Advances in Applied Energy* (2025); arXiv:2504.00937 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.00937) [VERIFIED-WEB]: definitions and evaluation criteria are unsettled; purely data-driven models "suffer from limited generalization ability and a lack of physical consistency." Parallel review: Ma, Jiang et al., "A review of physics-informed machine learning for building energy modeling," *Applied Energy* (2025) (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0306261924025534) [REPORTED; publisher blocks robots]. A field whose reviews are titled "nascent" is a field with no incumbent product moat on the model side — the moat opportunities are in DATA and VALIDATION INSTRUMENTS, not in model weights [INFERRED].

#### §1.1.2 Control on top of models: what real deployments actually deliver

- **The decisive field review.** Khabbazi et al., "Lessons learned from field demonstrations of model predictive control and reinforcement learning for residential and commercial HVAC: a review," *Applied Energy* 399 (2025); arXiv:2503.05022 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.05022) [VERIFIED-WEB]. 104 field demonstrations reviewed (24 residential, 80 commercial). **71 % "use experiment protocols that may lead to unreliable performance estimates."** The reliable ~29 % deliver weighted-average COST savings of **16 % residential / 13 % commercial**. **Only 13 of 104 papers report deployment/operation/maintenance costs at all.** Three charter-relevant readings: (i) the value of a good home thermal model is real and now has a defensible number attached; (ii) the field's evidentiary discipline is weak — a variance-banded, honestly-protocoled measurement culture is a differentiator IN THE LITERATURE ITSELF (the SP-4 thesis from the L-E return §2.4, independently corroborated from the model side); (iii) the unpriced deployment-cost hole is exactly the "modeling effort" barrier the classic MPC review named (Drgoňa et al., "All you need to know about model predictive control for buildings," *Annual Reviews in Control* 50:190–232 (2020) [REPORTED]).
- **Model-based RL for buildings exists and is simulation-heavy.** E.g., "Multi-zone HVAC control with model-based deep reinforcement learning," arXiv:2302.00725 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.00725) [REPORTED]; the residential RL validations run against BOPTEST-class simulators with occasional real-case checks (e.g., https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10791-025-09544-y [REPORTED]). No surveyed work runs a LEARNED world model as the operational truth source in a deployed home — learned models propose setpoints; they do not adjudicate state [VERIFIED-WEB by absence across this lane's searches; bounds at §4.3].

#### §1.1.3 Occupancy and presence: the best-evidenced home inference, and the clearest honesty hazard

- **Physics-prior occupancy works and is old.** Candanedo & Feldheim, "Accurate occupancy detection of an office room from light, temperature, humidity and CO2 measurements using statistical learning models," *Energy and Buildings* 112:28–39 (2016) (https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0378778815304357; dataset: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/357/occupancy+detection) [REPORTED] — ~95–99 % detection accuracy in an instrumented office. The CO₂ mass-balance lineage continues into multi-zone mechanically-ventilated estimation (e.g., "Applicability and accuracy of physical CO2 mass balance in multi-zone… office buildings to estimate real-time occupancy," 2026, https://www.researchgate.net/publication/398279122 [REPORTED]). Caveat the charter should hold: accuracies are room-scale, instrumented, and label-supervised — none of this transfers to "the platform knows who is home" without a consent story and error bars.
- **At consumer scale the evidence source is thermostat telemetry.** The ecobee "Donate Your Data" program: "more than 200,000 homes" sharing anonymized thermostat data with named researchers (Carleton, ACEEE, BC CDC, Berkeley Lab, EPA) (https://www.ecobee.com/en-us/donate-your-data/) [VERIFIED-WEB]. Data shape, from a paper built on it: 5-minute sampling of setpoints, per-stage runtime, PIR motion, indoor and outdoor temperature, across ~27,764 thermostats in the 2019 snapshot (Kane & Sharma, "Data-driven identification of occupant thermostat-behavior dynamics," arXiv:1912.06705, https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.06705) [VERIFIED-WEB]. This is the field's largest real-home behavioral/thermal corpus, it exists because a VENDOR's install base donated it, and DOE mirrors a 1,000-home subset as a public dataset (https://www.osti.gov/dataexplorer/biblio/dataset/1854924 [REPORTED; OSTI fetch errored this lane]). The structural read: in this category, PROPRIETARY LOGS ARE THE SCARCE RESEARCH ASSET — the moat-relevant fact for any event-sourced platform [INFERRED].
- **RF sensing is the new occupancy substrate and its literature is adjacent, not home-semantic.** Wi-Fi CSI sensing at consumer scale is PROVEN and standardized (IEEE 802.11bf, published 2025-09) — held as fenced ground from the Substrate Thesis §4.1 and the L-E return §4.5 (including the KIT ~99.5 % person-identification-from-CSI result and the standard's refused privacy mechanism); not re-derived here. What this lane adds: the SHIPPED consumer instances are §1.2.4's product facts.
- **Sensor-fusion physics priors are shipped hobbyist practice, not product.** Home Assistant's Bayesian integration — "a virtual binary sensor that determines its state by combining the states of other sensors using probability," documented for exactly the inference use-cases at issue ("cooking, showering, being in bed"), with explicit prior/threshold configuration (https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/bayesian/) [VERIFIED-WEB]. The field's largest platform ships the MECHANISM and leaves the semantics, calibration, and honesty surface to each user — the commercialization gap in one artifact.

#### §1.1.4 Real deployments and living labs (where "real" actually means real)

- **Empa NEST / UMAR (Switzerland):** occupied living-lab units used for experimental data-driven and RL control (e.g., "Data-driven predictive control for demand side management: theoretical and experimental results," *Applied Energy* (2023), https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306261923014654 [REPORTED]; "Price-responsive control using deep reinforcement learning for heating systems: simulation and living lab experiment," *Energy* (2025), https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0360544225041593 [REPORTED]). This is the PCNN data lineage — the strongest single site where physics-informed home-scale models met occupied reality.
- **CASAS (Washington State University):** the long-running smart-home activity-recognition testbed program ("CASAS: A smart home in a box," *IEEE Computer* (2013), https://eecs.wsu.edu/~cook/pubs/computer12.pdf [REPORTED]), with raw motion/door/activity-label corpora still being reissued on Zenodo in 2025 (https://zenodo.org/records/16343844 [REPORTED]). Scale: tens of testbeds, event-log-shaped data — the closest academic analog to "models over a home event log," and the hardware class is trivially low (binary ambient sensors).
- **The utility-scale disaggregation deployment:** Bidgely passed "10 million homes under contract" for meter-data disaggregation in 2017 (BusinessWire, 2017-12-12, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20171212005478/en/ [REPORTED]) — physics-signature inference sold at scale, but to UTILITIES, from meter data, in the cloud. The buyer was never the household.

#### §1.1.5 The frontier world-model wave: none of it lands in the home

- **V-JEPA 2** — Meta, 2025-06-11: 1.2 B-parameter video world model, 1 M+ hours of pretraining video, "zero-shot planning and robot control," 65–80 % pick-and-place success on unseen objects. No home, building, or ambient application named (https://ai.meta.com/blog/v-jepa-2-world-model-benchmarks/) [VERIFIED-WEB].
- **Genie 3** — Google DeepMind, 2025-08: interactive generated environments ("a new frontier for world models," https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-3-a-new-frontier-for-world-models/) [REPORTED] — a simulation substrate, not a deployed-space model.
- **Cosmos 3** — NVIDIA, 2026-05-31: "the world's first fully open omnimodel," mixture-of-transformers over text/image/video/ambient-sound/actions "with physics accuracy"; named domains are robotics, autonomous vehicles, and industrial/smart-space vision AI; the home is absent (https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-launches-cosmos-3-the-open-frontier-foundation-model-for-physical-ai) [VERIFIED-WEB].
- The survey layer confirms the aim-point: world-model surveys organize around robotics, driving, and games (e.g., "World model for robot learning: a comprehensive survey," arXiv:2605.00080 [REPORTED]; the ACM CSUR 2025 world-models survey, https://github.com/tsinghua-fib-lab/World-Model [REPORTED]).
- **The home-side academic wave is benchmarks, not deployments** — the L-E return's SMH-Bench row (arXiv:2606.01912: 1,100 tasks, 135-device SIMULATED homes, frontier models failing state-dependent tasks) is the fenced citation of record; nothing this lane found contradicts it, and the next-event-prediction literature over home logs remains small-corpus and pre-commercial (e.g., "Prediction of the next sensor event and its time of occurrence in smart homes," Springer LNCS (2019), https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-30490-4_37 [REPORTED]; "Predicting user behavior in smart spaces with LLM-enhanced logs and personalized prompts," arXiv:2412.12653 [REPORTED]).
- **Negative result, stated as the brief requires:** across this lane's searches, no paper or product was found that runs a general learned world model over a real deployed home's event stream as an operational layer. The field's world models live where labeled video and simulators live — robotics and AVs — and the home-adjacent entries are benchmarks and single-channel models. An empty field is a finding: the seed would be EARLY, not late, with everything that means in both directions [VERIFIED-WEB by absence; bounds §4.3].

#### §1.1.6 The refutation file: evidence AGAINST model-layer value in homes

- **Brandon, Clapp, List, Metcalfe & Price, "The human perils of scaling smart technologies: evidence from field experiments," NBER Working Paper 30482 (2022)** (https://www.nber.org/papers/w30482) [VERIFIED-WEB]: across 18 months, 16 M+ hourly electricity observations and ~4 M thermostat-interaction events, "little evidence that smart thermostats have a statistically or economically significant effect on energy use" — because "user behavior dampens energy savings," i.e., people override the model. Set against ENERGY STAR's own field-metric certification (≥8 % heating / ≥10 % cooling runtime reduction at the lower 95 % confidence limit, ~"$50 per year," computed from real-home telemetry samples; https://www.energystar.gov/products/heating_cooling/smart_thermostats/smart_thermostat_faq) [VERIFIED-WEB], the two are reconcilable: certification measures devices' runtime metrics on retained users; the RCT measures the causal effect INCLUDING the humans. The synthesis this lane files: **model accuracy is not the deployed bottleneck — occupant trust, comprehension, and override behavior are.** For a platform whose differentiation IS the explanation surface ("why did it fire / why didn't it"), this is the single most strategy-relevant empirical result in the sweep [INFERRED, so labeled].
- **NILM's accuracy-in-lab vs value-in-home gap** is the same shape: the deployment-pathway review (Energies 19(8):1883 (2026), https://www.mdpi.com/1996-1073/19/8/1883 [REPORTED]) catalogs scenario-specific failure modes; and the edge-resource study (§1.3) shows the good models miss Pi-class real-time. Meanwhile the shipped survivors (Sense, Bidgely) sell ALERTS AND ITEMIZATION, not control.
- **The trust ledger (§1.4.3)** — hallucinated occupants at the largest platform, a 13,000-customer camera cross-exposure, a home humanoid whose fallback is a human teleoperator — is market evidence that UNGROUNDED inference is actively destroying the category's trust while GROUNDED, narrow inference (Ting/Phyn class) is being paid for. Both halves of that sentence are the house thesis in market data [INFERRED from §1.4.3's cited facts].

### §1.2 The field: what actually ships or is concretely announced (mechanisms, dated)

Sense uses machine learning to identify devices" (https://sense.com/technology) [VERIFIED-WEB]; sold as insight/alerts, increasingly embedded meter-side. #### §1.2.3 What the pattern proves and does not prove
Across §1.2.1–1.2.2: every commercial success is (i) ONE physical channel, (ii) a NAMED hazard or bill, (iii) inference LABELED as alert/estimate with a learn-in period, (iv) an accountable buyer (insurer, utility, energy spend), and usually (v) dedicated sensing hardware. What no one ships: cross-device, cross-channel home-state inference sold as a platform property. The nearest admission of the gap is the field's own largest open platform: Home Assistant's `assumed_state`/`expected_state` architecture discussion — consensus reached, unimplemented for 4+ years — the L-E return §4.1's fenced citation, unchanged as of its 2026-08-15 verification.

RF-physics inference: shipped, productized, €1/month.

Mass-market privacy alarm followed in the press (Tom's Guide / Tom's Hardware coverage, 2025 [REPORTED: https://www.tomsguide.com/computing/online-security/xfinity-just-added-wi-fi-powered-motion-tracking-to-its-routers-heres-why-it-could-be-a-privacy-nightmare]) — the consent gap arriving on schedule (Substrate Thesis Q9; L-E §4.5).

### §2.1 Where it would sit, and what it would consume from the log as it exists today

**Layer answer, unambiguous on the evidence:** a physics/world-model capability is an **L2 occupant** — semantic/probabilistic judgment over physical state — REGARDLESS of how much physics structure it carries. PCNNs' "physically consistent by design" (§1.1.1) constrains the hypothesis class; it does not make outputs observations. The moat note's §2 line is the governing distinction: a model, however physics-shaped, is a REASONER; the log is the WITNESS. The seed's own P1–P3 (report physics, link physics, sensor plausibility) are different: they are L1-ENRICHMENT — evidence about the evidence channel — which is why the L-E return could recommend P1-A as bench method without touching the model question. RS-2's subject — thermal models, occupancy inference, behavior prediction — sits strictly ABOVE the floor: it PROPOSES; the harness enforces. Nothing in this sweep argues for physics inference below the enforcement boundary, and §1.1.6's override evidence argues actively against letting model outputs gate anything.

**What a first physics layer would consume from the event log / state store that EXISTS TODAY (AS-BUILT per L-E §1):**
- the totally-ordered, hash-chained `state_reported` stream with timestamps — the training/fitting substrate for any per-home model (the north star's "state, audit record, and dataset," with the dataset clause becoming operative);
- the availability regimes' outputs and persisted recency (per-device last-evidence times) — the staleness context any honest inference must carry;
- the command→`state_confirmed` pairs from the pending-command ledger — labeled actuation outcomes, which are exactly the "action-conditioned" data real-world model learning needs and almost no home dataset has;
- the bench's measured-cadence corpus (rig-scope, n=1) — per-device evidence-budget priors for weighting observations.

**What it would NEED that does not exist today (each item = a charter-visible cost, not a given):**
1. **Numeric environmental channels at density.** Thermal/occupancy modeling needs temperature/humidity/CO₂-class streams in the log. Whether the current device corpus records ANY numeric environmental channel at fitting-grade density is **UNVERIFIED at HEAD by this lane** — the single highest-value one-grep verification the charter can commission (§3.3-Q1). The 5-minute DYD shape (§1.1.3) is the field's proof that this density suffices when present.
2. **Outdoor/weather context.** Every thermal result cited consumes outdoor temperature. A local-first weather ingest (or an honest declared absence) is a new integration surface with its own provenance discipline — external data entering the log must be labeled as imported context, never as observed home state [INFERRED; the honesty law applied].
3. **Per-device measured envelopes as stored contracts** — the P1-A/P1-B loop the L-E return already priced (its §1.1 SHAPES); a model layer would be their second consumer.
4. **Energy telemetry** — absent entirely (L-E §1.3: the bench plug is the no-energy variant); any Sense/NILM-class ambition is hardware-first.
5. **Ground-truth occupancy labels for any supervised claim** — the consent-heavy item; the literature's accuracies (§1.1.3) all ride labels we do not have and should not casually collect (the founding-premise fence: opt-in or it does not exist).

**The asset statement, honestly bounded:** the event-sourced record is a REAL structural advantage for this direction — replay determinism makes model evaluation counterfactual-grade (train/test on the same immutable history; the north star's replay property doing research work), and command→confirmation pairs are rare data. But on today's channels the log is evidence-RICH about actuation and evidence-THIN about environment. The honest formulation for the charter: **the substrate is the right BENCH for a physics layer; whether it yet holds the right DATA is an open, checkable fact** [INFERRED + UNVERIFIED item 1].

### §2.3 The moat question, argued both directions (the charter rules)

**COMPOUND — the case that a physics layer multiplies the substrate bet:**
1. **It is the dataset clause becoming operative.** The log's third identity ("dataset") does no strategic work until something consumes it. A per-home model layer is the first consumer that makes every additional device-day of ordered history more valuable — the DYD lesson (§1.1.3) at platform scale: the corpus is the scarce asset, and ours is per-home, consented, and locally held [INFERRED from cited facts].
2. **It compounds with model progress instead of being consumed** — the instruction-vs-evidence distinction holds: better external models make a truthful, physics-annotated context substrate MORE valuable (better proposers, same witness), while our layer's own value rides structure + local data, not weights (the moat note §2; LE-C1's hub asymmetry re-confirmed at §1.3).
3. **Counter-positioning is structural, per-competitor.** Data-funded platforms cannot honestly sell "your home's model, held locally, labeled as inference" without contradicting their economics; the narrow-physics winners (Ting/Phyn) are single-channel by design of their business model. The position is copy-awkward for each named class for ITS OWN reason (§1.4.3's synthesis; the Substrate Thesis §2.3 discipline).
4. **The validation instrument is the rare part.** §1.1.2: 71 % of the field's own demonstrations have unreliable protocols. We own a variance-banded measurement culture and a replay-deterministic evaluation bench — the exact scarcity. A model layer built HERE could be the first with honest error bars at fleet scale (SP-4's category-empty finding, consumed from the model side).

**DILUTE — the case that it is a second unproven claim surface:**
1. **The multiplier law binds:** the moat compounds only if v1 ships (the seed §5; L-E §5.1's steelman). Every physics-layer hour pre-v1 is spent on futures whose value is conditional on a ship date this work does not advance — and A-14's 15 h/wk floor makes the tradeoff zero-sum.
2. **The market prices the position near zero today.** W-MARKET F-1: enforcement/evidence reads as overhead in current platform behavior; §1.4.2's WTP curve anchors occupant-paid inference at €1–20/month with the giants bundling it toward free. The liability channel pays — but for PREVENTED LOSSES, not for evidence properties as such (the moat note §5's unmoved verdict).
3. **Inference is where the honesty brand goes to die if rushed.** One over-claimed prediction surface costs more brand than ten honest verdicts earn (the never-retract bar); §1.1.6 shows even ACCURATE models lose to human trust dynamics. The differentiator is the discipline, and discipline spent on a second claim surface is discipline not spent hardening the first.
4. **The n=1 rig bounds every model claim harder than it bounds evidence claims.** A cadence envelope is defensible at n=1 with rig-scope declared; "our thermal model predicts YOUR home" is a per-home generalization claim with no fleet behind it — the hostile-reviewer attack (L-E §6.4) lands harder on the model side.
5. **Emptiness cuts both ways.** §1.2.6's unoccupied intersection may be unoccupied because the general home world model is a product nobody needs at consumer prices — the narrow winners suggest value concentrates in single channels with liability payers, and a platform-general layer may be a category error until an enterprise/insurer buyer exists [the refutation-welcome reading of the same absence].

**Net, as analysis (the charter rules):** the COMPOUND case is architecturally true and the DILUTE case is commercially true TODAY. They are reconciled by sequencing, not by choosing: the compounding is real only over a shipped v1 and a measured corpus; the dilution is real only if inference surfaces ship before measurement backs them. That reconciliation is exactly option A's shape — and if the charter judges even that scope a distraction from v1, option B loses almost nothing PROVIDED the tripwires are armed, because the structural moat (ordered, replayable, honest evidence) does not decay while we wait; only the CLAIMS window does (L-E §4.4's 12–24-month claims-vs-mechanism split).
