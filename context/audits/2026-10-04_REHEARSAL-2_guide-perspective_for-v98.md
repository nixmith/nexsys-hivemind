# An independent perspective for v98 — what tonight's rig says, and what the trajectory says

## §1 What tonight actually measured (the strategic reading; the adjudication is the return's)

1. **The availability model on `5b0e20c` cannot see a dark-but-cached device.** The sensor read `AVAILABLE stale=False` for 18 min of radio silence after the restart (BOOT2 first heard it at 22:49:45Z; relinked from cache at 22:31:55Z). The API's `lastReported` "advanced" past the earlier read only because the pre-restart report was newer — a clause that HELD while the device was dark. J1 (`availability_changed` v2, the 60-s mains probe, `lastSeenAt` on the wire) is precisely the fix, and it is not on the Pi. The most valuable single act in the week is getting J1 onto the Pi and reading the soak on it. The plan already does this (BC8 → soak). Keep it first.
2. **A power cycle is invisible to the join layer — on two device classes.** G4-1 (a router plug) and the SNZB-06P24 (after its 17:20 re-seat) both came back with no `device_join`/`device_announce`/`device_relinked` line, while frames and reports resumed within ~2 min. Whether the devices send no Device_annce or the adapter logs it only on the trust-center path is a source question for the hub; either way the detector for "a device went away and came back" is the link/report layer, not the join layer. That makes IR-56's remaining arm (a cached, un-adopted device that resumes silently at runtime is never re-proposed — J2a covers only the boot case) and IR-118 (an adopted device dark under a cache relink while the digest reads green) two consumers of one detector: the first frame, or its absence, at the link layer. J1's probe and `availability_link` already are that detector for adopted devices; the same hook fired for a cached, un-adopted device is IR-56's re-proposal trigger. One unit, one instrument, two consumers — and most of it is a test and a grader row, not new runtime code.
3. **The log and the store disagree about a window's close.** The store carries `permit_join_closed=1`; the log carries no close line of any spelling (BOOT1 whole). For a product whose thesis is "the event log is state, audit record and dataset", a store/log disagreement is a first-class bug class even when the fix is one INFO line. The deeper lesson is for the bench: several packet EXPECTs grep the log for facts the store holds (`closed=1`; the relink rows). Where the store has the event, the harvest should read the store. That one rule would have removed two of tonight's three guide departures.
4. **The 54-hour dark span was very probably a chewed cable.** The record spent parts of several beats pre-registering radio hypotheses for it (the 25-h arm, the pairing gesture). A physical-first rule — a device dark > 24 h gets one look at power and LED before any radio hypothesis is pre-registered — costs Nick thirty seconds and would have saved the hub most of a day's framing. Tonight's P2 cause-clause was refuted by the same fact: the sensor joined on the window alone, 13 min after re-power, LED dark; the gesture (re-seat) was never tested.
5. **Nothing broke.** `formed=0` all night; the restart reached `integration.launched` in 5 s; `relinked=10`; plug loads identical across the restart; the restore byte-identical. The core is more robust than the planning around it implies. That is a good sign — and the argument that the rehearsal class can be lighter (§5.12).
