You report; the hub adjudicates. Read the north-star document FIRST, in full.

Also test the document's meta-claim that the field is "trending away from deterministic methods" — find 2–3 additional 2024–2026 papers that either corroborate or cut against it.

**Charge 2 — EU AI Act Article 12, at the statute.** Quote the operative Article 12 text (and any adjacent provisions on record-keeping/logging for high-risk systems and agentic workflows).
