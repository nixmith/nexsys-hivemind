## §2 The lane's method (binding)
1. **Start from the purposes, not the catalog.** Every candidate unit must name the ROW it serves and the INSTRUMENT that will read it: ENERGY-READ (the platform's watts vs a reference) · the corroboration law of the run (a confirmation corroborated by a plug's own watts) · LINK-READ (nothing to buy) · IR-15's verification after the fix (a second IAS zone type) · the next device classes on the ambition map (illuminance 0x0400 — IR-18; presence/mmWave; a second-vendor plug for generality). A unit with no row and no instrument is not a candidate — it is a wish (the ambition map's own rule).
2. **The scientific setup Nick asked for, made concrete:** (a) **two or more plug MODELS from two or more VENDORS**, standard ZCL only (0x0B04 and/or 0x0702 as server clusters; `acPowerDivisor`/`multiplier` present), so ENERGY-READ is proven on a divisor of 1 AND a divisor of 10 (or whatever the two report) — one vendor proves a quirk, two prove the read path; (b) **one reference meter of Class 0.5 or better** (the P3 P4460 at ±0.2 % qualifies; the PN2000 at Class 1.0 is the fallback) — state each candidate's accuracy CLASS and resolution, from the maker's page; (c) **a known, stable resistive load** that the platform can be asked about — an incandescent or halogen bulb of a labeled wattage, or a small resistive heater at a fixed setting — with the reasoning for its stability (a resistive load's power varies with the square of line voltage; a 120 V nominal at ±5 % moves a 60 W lamp by ±10 % — say so, and pick a load whose reference reading is taken at the same instant as the platform's); (d) **repeatability**: the same load on each plug, read by the same meter, in one sitting — the design of the bench scenario's operator blocks, sketched in ≤10 lines so the bench lane can write the YAML.
3. **Grade every candidate on the same table** (§4). Cite the maker's page for clusters and ratings; cite z2m's converter (at a TAG, e.g. `zigbee-herdsman-converters` `v25.80.0`) for the divisor/quirk shape; cite ONE US retailer page for price and stock (dated). No claim without a link; a claim you cannot fetch is written as UNVERIFIED in §5 of your return, never dropped silently.
4. **Refuse in writing:** any second protocol (Matter, Thread-only, Z-Wave, Wi-Fi-only) — one radio until the run; Tuya-class plugs whose `manufacturerName` variants change clusters (unless one variant is pinned by z2m and sold under a stable listing); any device the IAS fence blocks before IR-15; anything whose only reason is "future" with no row this quarter.
5. **Two tiers, priced:** CORE (what the six weeks strictly need beyond what Nick owns) and EXTENDED (the next two device classes on the map, bought now only if the price of waiting is higher than the price of the unit). Nick chooses with one word.
