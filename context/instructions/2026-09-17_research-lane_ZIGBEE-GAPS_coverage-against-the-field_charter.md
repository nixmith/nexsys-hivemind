# ZIGBEE-GAPS — coverage against the field, with an instrument per gap

## §0 The question, in one line
Of the Zigbee devices people actually buy, what fraction would this platform read, classify and adopt correctly at HEAD — measured, not guessed — and what are the ten gaps whose closing moves that fraction the most per lane-day?

- **Cluster handlers — EIGHT:** `integration/integration-zigbee/…/ClusterHandlers.java:36–:52` registers OnOff 0x0006, LevelControl 0x0008, ColorControl 0x0300, OccupancySensing 0x0406, PowerConfiguration 0x0001, TemperatureMeasurement 0x0402, RelativeHumidity 0x0405, IasZone 0x0500. NOT registered: ElectricalMeasurement 0x0B04, Metering 0x0702 (ENERGY-READ, chartered next), Illuminance 0x0400 (IR-18), Pressure 0x0403, Flow 0x0404, Thermostat 0x0201, WindowCovering 0x0102, DoorLock 0x0101, FanControl 0x0202, Identify 0x0003, Groups/Scenes 0x0004/0x0005, Alarms 0x0009, Multistate/Analog I/O, Tuya EF00, and every manufacturer-specific cluster.
- **Reporting defaults:** `ReportingConfigurator.java:65–:72` knows 0x0406, 0x0B04 (`activePower` 0x050B, min 5 s, max 3600 s, change 10 — RAW units, the divisor not applied: DEVICE-SET's finding), 0x0702 (`currentSummationDelivered`, change 5), and the rows above them for the eight handled clusters.
- **Device-type classification:** `EndpointClassifier.java` maps by ZLL/HA device type — `:41` `DEVICE_TYPE_SMART_PLUG = 0x0051` (→ SWITCH + `on_off`); `:39` 0x0107 occupancy sensor (`:95`); the IAS zone capability `:158/:175` (IR-15: every non-CONTACT zone → `motion`). **A metering plug that identifies as On/Off Plug-in Unit 0x010A (the Shelly Plug US Gen4 — DEVICE-SET) does not reach the plug branch** — classification by device type alone misses devices whose clusters say more than their type.
- **Adoption paths proven on hardware (the claim register):** C-002 — a rejoining mains device through the rejoin path inside an operator-opened join window; C-003 — a sleepy battery end device through the ZDO second surface (`ieee_addr_req`/`rsp`). Not proven: an install-code (Zigbee 3.0) join; Touchlink; a device that needs a manufacturer-specific interview step (Tuya's magic packet; Xiaomi/Aqara's `0xFCC0`); a router-only rejoin without a join window.
- **Link quality:** `EzspIncomingMessage` parses `lastHopLqi`/`lastHopRssi` and drops them (IR-16; LINK-READ).
- **The register rows that already name gaps:** IR-15 (IAS zone types), IR-16 (link quality), IR-17 (no handler for 0x0B04/0x0702; the 0x0051-only plug branch), IR-18 (Illuminance 0x0400), IR-20 (the interview's cluster list neither logged nor persisted; the per-frame path), IR-21 (no runtime log-level knob). ## §2 The method (binding)
1. **The corpus is the measure.** Clone or fetch `Koenkk/zigbee-herdsman-converters` at ONE tag (name it; `v26.110.0` is the newest DEVICE-SET cited) and count, over `src/devices/*.ts`: definitions per vendor; the frequency of each `extend`/`fromZigbee` capability (onOff, light, electricityMeter, occupancy, illuminance, temperature/humidity, iasZone types, cover, lock, thermostat, battery, …); the frequency of each device type and each cluster where the definitions expose them; the share of definitions that carry a manufacturer-specific quirk (custom converters, `configure` steps, Tuya datapoints). If the repo is too large to fetch whole, sample by directory and say the sample. A device "category" is a capability set the corpus makes common.
2. **Coverage = the share of corpus definitions whose EVERY exposed capability HEAD reads, classifies and can adopt** — computed per category and overall, with the assumptions stated (what "adopt" means at HEAD: the C-002/C-003 paths). Report three numbers: read (a handler exists), classify (`EndpointClassifier` reaches the right branch), adopt (a proven join path). A category HEAD reads but mis-classifies (the 0x010A plug) is a separate row from one it cannot read at all.
3. **The gap list — ranked by corpus frequency × the size of the fix,** each row with: the gap · the corpus share it blocks · the source line(s) that would change · the INSTRUMENT that proves it closed (a bench scenario, a unit test over a recorded frame, a real device on the rig) · the device on the rig or in DEVICE-SET's list that exercises it · the dependency (IR row, fence, ENERGY-READ/LINK-READ) · a lane-day estimate. Ten rows minimum; more if the curve is flat.
4. **The market column, narrow:** for the top ten categories by corpus frequency, name the ONE best-selling US device per category (a retailer page, dated) and whether it is standard ZCL or quirk-bound — that is the "study the smart home market" Nick asked for, kept to what changes a gap's rank. No comparative product claims; the claim register's §0 preamble binds every sentence that could leave the building.
5. **Refuse in writing:** Matter/Thread as a second radio (the run's fence); Tuya EF00 datapoint devices as a class UNLESS the corpus share is so large that refusing it caps coverage below what the plan needs — then say the number and stop; anything with no instrument.
6. **Read-set:** the ambition map; `context/research/2026-09-15_DEVICE-SET_return.md` §1 and §5; `context/planning/improvement-register.md` IR-15..IR-21; `homesynapse-core-docs/design/08-*` (the cluster map Doc 08 §3.5); the four source files in §1, whole; `context/strategy/claim-register.md` §0.
