## §4 Technical specification
### §4.1 The probe at INFO (IR-137) — `ZigbeeIntegrationAdapter.evaluateAvailabilityTimeouts`
The line keeps its token and gains the fields that decide the cause: `zigbee.availability_ping: device={} nwk=0x{} ep={} outcome={} rttMs={} seen_during_probe={}` at **INFO** (≤ 10 probes/min at the run fleet by `MAINS_PING_SILENCE`'s derivation; in practice a few per night). `nwk` is the four-hex network address the exchange used (`%04X`), `ep` the endpoint `firstApplicationEndpoint` chose; on the `ERROR` arm both print `-`. **`seen_during_probe`** is the discriminator, and it CANNOT be read at the exchange's return: `zclGlobalExchange` (`Z/EzspCoordinatorProtocol.java:2009–:2037`) waits in `awaitIncomingLocked` (`:1874–:1908`), which ENQUEUES every non-matching callback (`:1906` → `enqueueCallbackLocked` `:937–:946`); nothing is ingested until the next cycle's `ingestion.processCycle()` (`:660`), so `lastSeen` is unchanged at the return (the review F2). **The line is therefore emitted ONE CYCLE LATER, after that drain:** the adapter keeps `private final Map<Long, PendingProbe> probesAwaitingDrain` (`record PendingProbe(Instant start, Instant end, PingOutcome outcome, int nwk, int endpoint)`); the sweep puts each probe there after its exchange and still calls `cache.recordEvidence` (on OK) and `recordCommandResult` immediately, as now; at the TOP of the next `evaluateAvailabilityTimeouts()` (which `runCycleOnce` calls after `processCycle()`, `:660` → `:665`), every pending probe is logged with `seen_during_probe = availabilityTracker.lastSeen(device).map(t -> t.isAfter(pending.start())).orElse(false)` and removed. `true` means a frame from this device was parked during the probe and ingested at the drain (reachable; the reply was not matched) — that same frame reset the miss counter (§4.3) and the device is no longer a candidate; `false` means the device was silent for the whole window. The 50-ms delay is named in the javadoc. No other token moves; the `rttMs` field is `Duration.between(start, end)` as now.

### §4.2 The mains silence limit from the device's reporting contract (IR-138; `AVAIL-LIMIT: b-metered`)
**The contract** is the reporting the Core configured on the device at interview (readback-verified then; not in memory at boot — §0b row 16), re-derived at evaluation time as a pure function: a new package-private method `Optional<Duration> contractMaxIntervalFor(List<EndpointDescriptor> endpoints, DeviceProfile profile, boolean meteringOnly)` on `ReportingConfigurator` walks EXACTLY the rows `configureDevice` would drive (row 18: a skip-profile → empty; per endpoint, per input cluster, the `DEFAULTS` or `METERING_ROWS` row with the profile's override applied by the same two paths; a row whose effective maximum is `REPORTING_OFF_MAX_INTERVAL` is skipped) and returns the SMALLEST effective maximum as a `Duration` — sending nothing, reading nothing from the wire. With `meteringOnly` true, only the `METERING_ROWS` clusters (0x0B04, 0x0702) count. The adapter gains `private Optional<Duration> mainsContractFor(IEEEAddress device)` = the cached record (`cache.device`) → its `endpoints()` + the profile resolved from `matchedProfileId` exactly as `:621–:626` does → the method, with `meteringOnly` set by the word; a record without endpoints (uninterviewed) → empty.
