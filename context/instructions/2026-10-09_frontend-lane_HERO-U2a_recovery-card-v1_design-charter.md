## §4 What to watch out for
- **The verb is the state.** "Not responding" says the hub asked twice and heard nothing; the fifth state says the hub never asked; "quiet" says it asked and was answered. A sentence that blurs who acted fails the honesty law and the review (P5).
- **`availability` is a STRING key on the frozen reads.** Every new value is a NEW camelCase key beside it (the four-constraint law: additive-only; the tri-state law for presence). FIELDS.md never proposes `availability.probe` or a nested object under an existing key.
- **IR-133's null.** A healthy, reporting mains device carries `availabilityReason=null · lastSeenAt=null · link=null` until its first transition; the card reads Reporting from `availability` + a moving `lastReported`, and its "since" from `lastReported` — never "unknown since never".
- **Instants are ISO strings** (`Instant.toString()`), parsed only via `format.parseInstant`; the card shows a relative time with the absolute one one expand away; the countdown is computed from `closesAt`, never from a local timer alone.
- **The gesture library does not exist** (the research's Q3: the field has none to borrow). The gesture card per class is written from the seam note and the bench's device notes (`nexsys-bench/corpus/devices/*.md`) where they hold a reset gesture; where they do not, the card says so and SPEC §12 asks. No gesture is invented.
- **The window is scoped.** The act's copy says the window is for this device only; it never offers an unscoped window; a foreign joiner's rejection is a calm sentence, not an alarm.
- **Three stage columns, one truth per column.** A sentence may only read fields its column's stage carries. The degraded today-form is a real design, not a placeholder.
- **The Hue (IR-112) is on the network and silent on reports.** It is the exhibit for "Quiet since <t> (asked, answered)" when a probe answers — and for the honest limit of the card: a device that answers pings and never reports is not "fine"; the sentence beneath says what the household can do (nothing tonight; the record's row).
