Today the grader reads every timeout as UNCONFIRMED (`grader.py:384`) and says nothing of why, while the run's fourth question rides this path (IR-88). This unit gives every timed-out command a CAUSE, decided from the store's own events by the rule below — pre-registered here, applied in order, never guessed:
1. **The expectation** `(a, v)`: `EXPECTATIONS[command_type]` (`:216`; a timeout carries no `state_confirmed` of its own). None → `unattributed`, reason `no-expectation`.
2. **The prior**: the newest event BEFORE the command's `command_issued` (by `global_position`) on the same `subject_ref` that is a `state_reported` with `attribute_key == a` (read its `value`) or a `state_confirmed` with `attribute_key == a` (read its `expected_value`). None in the export → `unattributed`, reason `no-prior-value`. An opaque `state_reported`/`state_confirmed` on the subject between the prior and the issue → `unattributed`, reason `opaque` (it may be a newer report of `a`).
3. **`bench`** — the prior matches `v` under `values_match` (`:734`): the attribute already held the commanded value when the command was issued, so the device had no edge to report. The bench asked for a confirmation that could not come (IR-51's three nights).
4. **Not dispatched** (no `command_dispatched` in the command's partition) → `unattributed`, reason `not-dispatched` — the device never received it; that is the core's path, a different question.
5. **`device`** — otherwise: the prior differs from `v` (a real edge was asked), the command was dispatched, and no `state_reported` on (subject, `a`) matching `v` arrived between the issue and the timeout.

Recorded beside the cause, never changing it: the timeout's delay from the issue (ms) and the window (`timeout_ms`); the prior's value, age (s) and event id; the dispatch delay (ms); the newest readable `command_result` outcome, if any; the count of in-window reports on (subject, `a`) that did NOT match (invariant (v) reads the same events and keeps its own flag); and the LATE report — the first matching `state_reported` on (subject, `a`) after the timeout, inside the export, with its delay in seconds, or `none` (IR-52's split: a late report is the 5-s window's question, IR-53, POST-RUN; `none` is no edge seen in the export). The cause words (`bench` · `device` · `unattributed`) and reasons (`no-expectation` · `no-prior-value` · `opaque` · `not-dispatched`) are a CLOSED set, emitted through one refusing function like `say()`/`outcome()` and walked by the selftest; they travel under their own keys (`cause`, `cause_reason`), never under `verdict` or `outcome` (the walk at `:749`). **No invariant changes:** every verdict word, every command's outcome and every count stays as it is; `verdict.json` gains ONE block (per timed-out command one row — the cause, the reason, the evidence fields — and the totals per cause, overall and per `subject_ref`), and `report.md` one section, "Timeouts by cause", one line per timeout.

## §4 What to watch out for
- The rule is the record's, not a theory (arc 28): no fourth cause, no weighting, no "likely". A case the five steps do not decide is `unattributed` with its reason.
- Only commands that classify UNCONFIRMED by the timeout arm (`:384`) get a cause — not those confirmed, failed, or made `unconfirmed` by a result (the core's precedence, `classify()` `:371`). `edge_open` and `carried_in` commands keep their rows and get no cause.
- The prior is the newest BEFORE the issue by `global_position`, on the SAME `subject_ref` and attribute; a report on another entity of the same device is not it. `values_match` is the comparator; read a boolean the way (v) does (`:686`–`:690`).
- "In-window" is from the issue to the timeout event, by `global_position`; "late" searches after the timeout to the export's end and stops at the first match.
- The whitelist block is the PJ-2/J2 form exactly — comment lines first, then the two strings; `whitelist_size` moves by two because it reads the tuple's length.
