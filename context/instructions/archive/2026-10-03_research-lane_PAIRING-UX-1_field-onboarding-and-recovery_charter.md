- **The six questions (one section each in the return; a question with no field answer says so — "unfound" is a finding):**
  - **Q1 — THE ADD FLOW.** For each of the four platforms (Home Assistant with ZHA · Zigbee2MQTT · SmartThings · the Hue bridge): the exact user sequence to add a Zigbee device — where the join window is opened, how long it stays open, whether the UI shows a countdown, whether the UI tells the user to trigger the device BEFORE or AFTER the window (the seam's target 3), and what the user sees during the interview (target 4: stages or a spinner).
  - **Q2 — RECOVERY AFTER A POWER LOSS.** For each platform: what happens to a mains or USB-powered device that loses power and returns — is it re-admitted silently, does it return in pairing mode and sit unseen (our IR-117), does the UI show it as unavailable and after how long (our IR-121: never), and what the user is told to do. Name the platform's dark-device instrument (an "unavailable after N s" setting, a last-seen column, a notification) and its default.
  - **Q3 — "PAIRED ELSEWHERE" AND THE RESET GESTURE.** How each platform handles a device already bonded to another hub (the seam's target 1): is a reset offered first, is the device-specific gesture shown, and does the platform distinguish a REJOIN from a RESET (our IR-114: a 5-s hold on a SNZB is a leave, three presses on a Shelly a re-announce). Does any platform ship a per-device gesture library, and where it lives (a quirks database, a device database, vendor pages).
  - **Q4 — FAILURE AND REMEDY.** What the user sees when a join fails on each platform — the literal strings if findable ("interview failed", "not found in inclusion mode", a timeout) — and whether a remedy is offered with the error (target 4) or only a code. Whether logs are one click away.
  - **Q5 — THE OPERATOR-PAIRED ALTERNATIVE.** What the field offers households that do not pair their own devices: installer modes, pre-paired kits, a "bring it to the hub" wizard that assumes a technician. What a pre-paired kit implies for the household's later additions. This is what our cohort gets until `WIZARD:` is ruled (`COHORT-PACE`, `KIT: company-first-three`).
  - **Q6 — REMOTE ACCESS BY DEFAULT (for W18).** For each platform: is the dashboard LAN-only out of the box; what remote access is offered (a vendor cloud relay, a paid tier, a self-hosted VPN recommendation, Tailscale/WireGuard guidance in the docs); what the household is told on day one. One paragraph per platform; no pricing of our own layers.
