# BUSFIX: b — the ruling of record (Nick, Sat 2026-09-12, verbatim)

## §1 The ruling, as written
Ruling: (b) — store-driven, in-order LIVE delivery. Sequenced after EXPLAIN-114a on the Java slot; the design note authored now, in parallel; (i)'s instrument stays live as the tripwire.

Why (b) and not (a): (a) removes the guard that produced the silent drop and replaces it with a dedup marker in the loop — but the dedup marker is the half of (b) that matters, and what (a) keeps is the part that was wrong: delivery order still depends on notification order, so any future path that notifies out of sequence reintroduces the same class with a different face. And the failure mode (a) admits is the one this system must never have. A silent drop is a run that did not happen; a duplicate `command_issued` is a device actuated twice. Between a bus that occasionally forgets and a bus that occasionally acts twice, the honesty law and every explainability claim we have made choose the first, and (b) chooses neither. (b) also makes the census exact by construction, which retires the checkpoint-lag caveat and simplifies TR-1b's driver for free.

Why not (c) alone: the instrument is already landed at `72efb42`, so (c) happens regardless — every push's `bus-soak` job is now a sample that can print `NOTIFY_SKIPPED_LIVE`. Choosing (c) as the decision would mean waiting a week for a mechanism you say is proven at source, on a class that has shown four reds in thirteen samples and lives on exactly the host shape the product ships on. The reds no longer block the gate, which is precisely why waiting would cost nothing visible and something real.

The sequencing, so this is not blocking: EXPLAIN-114a takes the Java slot first — it is ready today, small, and touches `core/automation` and `api/rest-api`, not the bus. While it runs, v71 writes (b)'s design note as the AMD to Doc 01 §3.4 and the coding instruction; (b) dispatches when EXPLAIN lands. HERO-1c and R-4c proceed on their own domains. If a `NOTIFY_SKIPPED_LIVE` line appears in a sample before (b) dispatches, it goes into the AMD as the measured exhibit; if kind E never fires across the next ten samples and REPRO-1 at K=200 under two carriers stays green, the ruling flips to (c) as you state — a mechanism that never fires under the instrument built to catch it is not proven, whatever the source reading says.

## §2 The acceptance bar, as written (binding on the AMD and the instruction)
What (b) must carry to be accepted, so v71 writes it in before the lane starts: the invariant in the AMD, stated once — every matching position is delivered to each LIVE subscriber exactly once, in position order, regardless of the order notifications arrive; the in-memory max-delivered as the source of truth for the read-forward, with the persisted checkpoint kept as the durable floor for restart, never as a delivery guard; the notification reduced to a wake hint plus a bounded idle tick, so a lost wake costs one empty page read, not a stalled run; red-first tests in event-bus for the two cases that matter — two publishers notifying out of order deliver both, in order, once; and the TRANSITION→LIVE flip delivers each position exactly once, the duplicate (a) feared; the existing replay and transition ITs untouched and green; the desk soak at K=200 under two carriers green; then twenty consecutive `bus-soak` job samples green with kind E at zero. The cost is the one you named, one to two desk-days plus the note, and it buys the property the bus was always supposed to have.
