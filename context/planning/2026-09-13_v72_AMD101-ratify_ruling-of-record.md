Third, the three edits v72 named are the right three. I authored §4 with two (Doc 01 §3.4 and §9) and missed the invariants register; v72 is right that INV-BUS-01 (Exactly-Once Delivery Per Subscriber, refined by AMD-42) is where the mechanism clause has to live, because AMD-101 strengthens that invariant in two ways — it adds "in position order", which exactly-once never promised, and it pins the mechanism (the cursor delivers, the checkpoint floors, the notification wakes).
