## §2 The record's premises CORRECTED by §1 (the instruction carries the corrected form)
- **"The scoped key alone rejects a neighbor's device" — INCOMPLETE.** A scoped transient key makes a foreign joiner FAIL (it cannot decrypt a hashed-TCLK transport key) but does not make the TC DENY it: under 0x0003 the association is allowed and the host sees `device_join … decision=USE_PRECONFIGURED_KEY`, then silence — no rejection to log (§1 row 5). The product event D-v94-24 asked for ("a device that is not yours tried to join") needs the TC's DENY, which `EZSP_DECISION_JOINS_USE_INSTALL_CODE_KEY (0x0010)` supplies: "Allow joins if there is an entry in the transient key table." J2b's scoped open writes **0x0013** (ALLOW_JOINS | ALLOW_UNSECURED_REJOINS | JOINS_USE_INSTALL_CODE_KEY); the un-scoped open keeps 0x0003 with the wildcard key (today's behavior, unchanged).
- **"The window closes itself" — TRUE for the key (300 s), FALSE for the policy and the MAC window.** Today nothing reverts the policy and the MAC permit runs to its own duration; a scoped window that ends must clear its key (0x006B), revert the policy to rejoins-only (0x0002) and send `permitJoin(0)` — the SDK's close does all three (§1 row 8). This is the explicit close D-v95-4 named; it is NEW NCP-facing behavior and a one-way door's neighbor (§5).
- **"J2a re-proposes at boot" — the site moved:** `A:372` → **`A:392`** (`rehydrateAdoptionMaps()` after `readAdoptAcceptList()` at :385). The spike's cites at `5b0e20c` are stale by +20 at the top of A and +49 below :1500; the instruction cites `df2bc62` only.
- **"The rejected-joiner event has no hook" — FALSE:** `I:500–502` already surfaces `DENY_JOIN` as a WARN with the joiner's EUI64; J2b promotes it to an event with the window's scope beside it, and the adapter's own scope check at `A:1661` covers the host side (a rejoin candidate outside the scope is logged, not scheduled).
