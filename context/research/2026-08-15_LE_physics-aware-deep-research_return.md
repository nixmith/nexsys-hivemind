## §0 Executive summary (≤ 1 page)

**The seed's central premise survives contact with the field and the literature — with one large caveat and one clock.** Across four research passes, no consumer smart-home company, product, or open-source project was found marketing "physics-aware," "evidence-based device state," verified execution, or measured device-physics envelopes; no shipping platform derives availability or confirmation semantics from a device's own declared or measured reporting physics (the field's availability models are flat per-power-class timeouts); and no literature was found that couples command-confirmation semantics to rate-limited device evidence or to mesh state [VERIFIED-WEB by absence, §1/§4 — absence is inherently non-conclusive]. The stack's availability regimes, confirmation ledger, and measured-cadence corpus are, on this evidence, ahead of both the field and the literature on the exact ground the seed names — AS-BUILT, cited in §1. The caveat: the in-house lead is P1-deep, P2-partial, P3-absent (§1.3), and it rests on an n=1 rig whose external validity a hostile reviewer attacks first. The clock: the adjacent SCITT territory moved again THIS WEEK (an AI-agent action-receipt profile, 97 pp., filed 2026-08-14 — §3.2), and the generic agent-provenance literature is crowding fast (§2.3).

**The five sub-problems, novelty-checked (§2):** SP-1 (confirmation under report-rate constraints) — framing UNOCCUPIED. SP-2 (staleness semantics) — metric space crowded (AoI/AoS), the availability-verdict + derivation-at-read framing unoccupied. SP-3 (provenance-carrying agent context) — CROWDED generically (CaMeL, FIDES, an execution-provenance survey, live SCITT agent drafts); the physical-evidence slice partially open and closing fast. SP-4 (HIL reliability benchmarking) — the category appears GENUINELY EMPTY; the bench can already manufacture most of what a first publication needs. SP-5 (physics-model device profiles) — declaration half crowded (WoT TD, SDF, DTDL, MUD, Matter ICD), the measured-and-bench-verified half unoccupied. Recommended order: **SP-4 → SP-1 → SP-5 → SP-2 → SP-3** (readiness × value × moat-leak; §2.6). SP-4 and SP-1 are the two where the s31 thread and the nightly corpus are literally the pilot study.

**The three leverage vectors (§3):** enterprise — the CRA Article-14 reporting regime starts 2026-09-11 (24 h/72 h clocks, confirmed at regulation text) and the AI-Act high-risk logging duties moved to Dec-2027, converging with CRA full application in the same quarter; insurers measurably pay for adjacent signals but NOBODY prices tamper-evident logs as such — white space, WTP unproven. All three vectors occupy the L1 evidence floor and compound with model progress; none is consumed by it (§3.4).

### §1.2 P2 — Link/network physics

**FIELD.**
- The boot-adjacent cold window is a mechanistically understood, community-documented, product-unaddressed phenomenon. Mechanism: Zigbee concentrator (many-to-one) routing stores source routes at the coordinator; a coordinator/NCP restart discards or stales them, and until the next MTORR cycle and fresh route records, outbound unicasts fail or eat discovery latency ([VERIFIED-WEB] Silicon Labs "Zigbee Routing Concepts," docs.silabs.com; mechanism-to-symptom link [INFERRED], well-supported). Symptom record: years of Zigbee2MQTT issues ("can not reach devices after restart" #1657, #1059, #4154, ember-driver #29902) and the Z2M FAQ's official advice to "wait a few minutes so that the Zigbee network can settle" ([VERIFIED-WEB] zigbee2mqtt.io/guide/faq + github issues).
- Observability primitives exist (EmberZNet neighbor/route tables with per-neighbor LQI and costs; Thread Network Diagnostics cluster 0x0035; OpenThread diagnostic TLVs; HA's 2026 Matter server ships a network visualization with per-link signal quality) — all diagnostic surfaces, none consumed as evidence context for verdicts ([VERIFIED-WEB] Silicon Labs UG100/EmberNeighborTableEntry docs; matter-survey.org/cluster/0x0035; openthread.io; home-assistant.io blog 2026-06-23).
- **Negative finding, both halves:** no commercial platform surfaces a "mesh warming" state to users after hub restart, and no literature was found coupling application-level command confirmation or command confidence to current mesh state ([VERIFIED-WEB by absence across targeted searches] — the WSN link-quality-estimation literature (Baccour et al. CSUR 2012; Srinivasan et al. TOSN 2010 burstiness) is routing/MAC-layer, not confirmation semantics).

**OURS.**
- AS-BUILT — the measurement: the bench measured the exact phenomenon the field only advises waiting through — the s31 confirm leg TIMED_OUT 4/4 at 0.772 s post-EMBER_NETWORK_UP and confirms in 143 ms–3.59 s at +40–55 s; the B3.3 suite-position fix (verified, fenced ground) is the structural response, and its rationale chain is on the record (bench `16e672d` constants.yaml; the B3.3 audit).

### §2.2 SP-2 — Staleness semantics

- NOVELTY: **partially occupied; the specific framing unoccupied.** The metric space is crowded: Age of Information is a thousands-of-papers subfield (Yates et al., IEEE JSAC 2021 survey), and "Age of Staleness" (Cosandal & Ulukus, arXiv:2605.16176, 2026) already refines AoI toward "when was the estimate last correct" for digital twins — the closest single item found. Bounded staleness as a read guarantee is owned by the database lineage (Pileus, SOSP 2013; Cosmos DB). Real-time-database absolute/relative validity intervals (Ramamritham lineage) are genuine 1990s prior art for evidence-recency windows and must be cited. NOT found anywhere: availability as a verdict *derived at read time* from per-device evidence-recency envelopes, with stale-but-usable as a first-class API state [VERIFIED-WEB per-item]. The grey literature documents the exact user-facing gap (HA community threads on "unavailable vs last known value").

NEW: little hardware; mostly formalization plus a corpus of regime transitions from the nightly record.
- VENUE: CIDR (opinionated semantics), EuroSys/Middleware, IoTDI; AoI-native venues (INFOCOM/ISIT) are a poor fit — they expect queueing analysis. A paper that does not position explicitly against AoI/AoS and RTDB validity intervals gets rejected as reinvention — the research pass's own caution, adopted here.
- VALUE: medium-high; certifies the C-2 availability position. Moat-leak: LOW — the position is already public-facing posture; the persisted-recency implementation detail is ordinary engineering once stated.
- **RECOMMENDATION: ADOPT (fourth in order) — class (iii) over (i).**
