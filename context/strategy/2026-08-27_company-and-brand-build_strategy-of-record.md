# The company-and-brand build — strategy of record (v1.2)

## §0 The frame: what kind of company this is

One founder at a ~15 h/wk floor (A-14), an agent-fleet operating system that is now PROVEN (hub + chartered lanes + two-layer audits; v56–v57 ran research, code, skills, brand, and legal lanes in parallel with zero discipline loss), a product whose first hardware-verified external claim was minted narrow 09-02 (C-001), an entity settled (NexSys LLC — the entity ruling), and a name decision at the door. The Substrate Thesis is the market frame: we occupy the layer that COMPOUNDS with model progress. The build strategy's job is therefore not to add fronts — it is to make the few fronts we run compound, and to spend Nick's scarce hours only where they are irreplaceable. **The brand IS the honesty made legible**: we market the claim register, the explain surface, and the verdict corpus — never adjectives.

**The two goals, register-shaped (A-5; ratified R-4 (a) · R-5 (a)).** *The household (the individual smart home).* SHORT-TERM: one home lived in on the SHIPPED artifact — which is exactly R-4b (the fleet re-adopted and an automation firing on the packaged build: the C-002 sentence), R-4.5/R-5 (the settle redesign; the nightly fence lifts by its own proof), and the FE honesty rows (§10-G the store-truth read path; §10-J unresolvable refs surface LOUDLY, `Current` never renders without evidence). LONG-TERM, in the register's grammar: *why did it fire / why didn't it / did it actually confirm* answered from a never-evicted log (B-s2's shape) · the four presence metrics measured in THIS home (C-s2's shape, generalized — the four-qualifier law on every sensing sentence) · zero cloud dependence (the counter-positioning artifacts are dated per the FOP §1.3 — Amazon removed on-device voice processing 2025-03-28; Belkin Wemo bricked 2026-01 — and are RE-VERIFIED at the primary before any register row). None of these is an adjective; each is a sentence with a refutable-by. *The company.* SHORT-TERM = P3's exit (Nov-25): a product, a name, a surface, a fleet story, all evidence-backed — with a live register behind it. LONG-TERM = Substrate §12, sharpened by A-1b (from 2027-01-20 in the EU the deterministic floor is the CHEAPER conformity path; every ML-enforcement competitor carries a notified-body tax we do not): hold the reference implementation AND the measured corpus of the enforcement floor when that floor becomes a purchase requirement — liability and insurance first, regulation later. The moat is not the code (Apache gives it away by design): it is the corpus (grows with every device, home, and model generation), the register (a company whose every public sentence is auditable), and the spine (one founder running many fronts without quality collapse). **The risk named plainly:** the field is building better judges and leaving the floor unbuilt — until a well-capitalized incumbent builds it. The D5 form is the tripwire (the day the floor is no longer MISSING, the story changes), and Matter 1.6's provisional ambient-sensing clusters (June 2026) are moldable NOW — the FOP's standards-presence rows are the cheapest way to be in that room.

## §1 The three compounding assets (everything below serves one of these; anything that serves none is cut)

1. **The evidence engine** — the bench, the verdict corpus (≈1,728 verdicts, zero false CONFIRM, accruing nightly), the CI artifact channel (versioned, hash-verifiable, install→boot→health on every push), and now the hardware-proof chain (R-1/R-2 → R-3 → R-4). This is the product's moat AND the marketing pipeline's raw material. **Its outward form is THE MEASURED CORPUS (A-1; ratified R-1 (b)):** device profiles admitted as declarative data and published with their bench verdicts (`confirmed|unconfirmed`, never rounded up), and — from P3 — presence quality measured in real homes on four first-class metrics (latency-to-detect · latency-to-clear · false-clear-while-stationary · false-hold-after-vacancy). The corpus is the community wedge; the explain surface is its demo face.
2. **The claim register** — the fence-lift protocol (H9) turned outward: every lifted claim is a dated, commit-hashed, evidence-pointed sentence. The register is the ONLY source any public surface may draw from (B-5 builds the story kit ON it; the live site's `ledger-gap-dossier.md:12` form is the template). A company whose every public sentence is auditable is a brand no incumbent can copy quickly — their marketing debt is our differentiation.
3. **The operating spine itself** — the hivemind + skills + packet/navigator patterns. Every new front (design, marketing, community, legal) enters as a CHARTERED LANE with a return, an audit, and a census — which is precisely what lets one founder run many fronts without quality collapse. The spine's token economy (a ~27 KB boot) is what makes lanes cheap enough to multiply.

## §3 The leverage doctrine (the answer to "optimize our time and effort; scale up")

**Nick's hours are the only non-renewable input. They go ONLY to the five irreplaceable classes:** (1) hardware acts (cards, radios, ⏺s), (2) one-word rulings on packaged decisions (H10 — an open-ended question to Nick is a hub defect), (3) signatures/filings/payments (entity, counsel, USPTO), (4) human relationships (counsel, native speakers, future early users), (5) the paste-block operator loop (~15–30 min/weekday, 4–6 h weekend-anchored). **Everything else is hub- or lane-work by default**, and the burden of proof is on keeping a task IN Nick's column, never on delegating it.

**The scaling rules (how the fleet grows without discipline loss):**
1. **Packet-first.** No Nick-hour is spent un-pre-staged: every operator act arrives as a self-contained packet with expected values and STOP-gates (proven: the card sitting, R-3a, the LLC navigator). New-front work (design, story kit, community) enters the same way — as briefs with censuses and returns, audited at full strength (capability never substitutes for verification).
2. **Parallel lanes, serial rulings.** Lanes run unattended and in parallel wherever their write-sets are disjoint (the five-lane afternoon of v56 b2 is the ceiling proof); decisions stay serialized through Nick in H10 form. Scale the number of lanes, never the number of decision-makers.
3. **The audit is the license to delegate.** The two-layer audit + census discipline is not overhead — it is the mechanism that makes a one-person company able to accept work from many agents. It scales by getting CHEAPER per lane (banked verdicts, pointer-form spines, the ~60 %-cheaper boot), not by being relaxed.
4. **Author ahead of need.** The hub keeps every next act pre-staged (the R-3b/R-4 pattern) so Nick's calendar, not the hub's throughput, is always the binding constraint — and idle hub beats go to the spine's own upkeep (W-SKILLS-5, rotations) that keeps future lanes cheap.
5. **New fronts open only at their gate.** Marketing opens at the register (post-R-4), identity at the word, community at R-10, sales at the fleet. A front opened before its gate produces un-backed words or un-audited work — both are debt, not progress.
6. **The outward-chain guarantee (E-1, v58).** No week without ≥1 outward-chain act (register → surface → audience). The engine must not only polish itself: a week that only polishes the engine is a missed week, however productive the polishing.

## §3.5 The capital posture (Nick's word, v57 beat 5 — banked as strategy)

**Bootstrap-first.** A dedicated five-figure fund (across two institutions, earmarked for the company and nothing else) is the P0–P3 budget — and it COVERS P0–P3 with headroom: the counsel wave (~$550/class USPTO ×2–4 classes + counsel's opinion), the trade-name registration, identity/design tooling, hardware for the fleet build-out, domains. The same fund carries the P3 liability quotes (E-3, v58): general + product liability, priced and bound as advised BEFORE the first hardware sale. **Credit is reserve, not plan. Equity is the most expensive capital the company has** — the OA's Class B pool (≤ 35 %, 12/36 vesting) exists for deliberate, post-proof conversations only; no investor conversation before the claim register holds the story (P2 exit), and none is required by this plan at all. **Financial hygiene from day one:** every company expense through the company account (FIN-1) · every personal-to-company transfer recorded as a member capital contribution in the LLC records · a receipts ledger built retroactively to formation (2025-07) and kept current — formation + report fees, hardware, domains, counsel, filings — because the startup/organizational-expense treatment at the first active tax year is a TAX-PREPARER question that is only answerable if the records exist (§195-class; not tax advice; the preparer conversation is calendared). **The veteran assets:** SDVOSB (SBA VetCert) and Louisiana LAVETBIZ certifications are real future options for the fleet/procurement era — a P2/P3 calendar row, verified with SBA/counsel, never a present dependency.
