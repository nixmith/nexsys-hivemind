# The company-and-brand build — strategy of record (v1)

## §0 The frame: what kind of company this is

One founder at a ~15 h/wk floor (A-14), an agent-fleet operating system that is now PROVEN (hub + chartered lanes + two-layer audits; v56–v57 ran research, code, skills, brand, and legal lanes in parallel with zero discipline loss), a product whose first hardware-verified external claim is ~72 hours away, an entity settled (NexSys LLC — the entity ruling), and a name decision at the door. The Substrate Thesis is the market frame: we occupy the layer that COMPOUNDS with model progress. The build strategy's job is therefore not to add fronts — it is to make the few fronts we run compound, and to spend Nick's scarce hours only where they are irreplaceable. **The brand IS the honesty made legible**: we market the claim register, the explain surface, and the verdict corpus — never adjectives.

## §1 The three compounding assets (everything below serves one of these; anything that serves none is cut)

1. **The evidence engine** — the bench, the verdict corpus (≈1,728 verdicts, zero false CONFIRM, accruing nightly), the CI artifact channel (versioned, hash-verifiable, install→boot→health on every push), and now the hardware-proof chain (R-1/R-2 → R-3 → R-4). This is the product's moat AND the marketing pipeline's raw material.
2. **The claim register** — the fence-lift protocol (H9) turned outward: every lifted claim is a dated, commit-hashed, evidence-pointed sentence. The register is the ONLY source any public surface may draw from (B-5 builds the story kit ON it; the live site's `ledger-gap-dossier.md:12` form is the template). A company whose every public sentence is auditable is a brand no incumbent can copy quickly — their marketing debt is our differentiation.
3. **The operating spine itself** — the hivemind + skills + packet/navigator patterns. Every new front (design, marketing, community, legal) enters as a CHARTERED LANE with a return, an audit, and a census — which is precisely what lets one founder run many fronts without quality collapse. The spine's token economy (a ~27 KB boot) is what makes lanes cheap enough to multiply.

## §3 The leverage doctrine (the answer to "optimize our time and effort; scale up")

**Nick's hours are the only non-renewable input. They go ONLY to the five irreplaceable classes:** (1) hardware acts (cards, radios, ⏺s), (2) one-word rulings on packaged decisions (H10 — an open-ended question to Nick is a hub defect), (3) signatures/filings/payments (entity, counsel, USPTO), (4) human relationships (counsel, native speakers, future early users), (5) the paste-block operator loop (~15–30 min/weekday, 4–6 h weekend-anchored). **Everything else is hub- or lane-work by default**, and the burden of proof is on keeping a task IN Nick's column, never on delegating it.

**The scaling rules (how the fleet grows without discipline loss):**
1. **Packet-first.** No Nick-hour is spent un-pre-staged: every operator act arrives as a self-contained packet with expected values and STOP-gates (proven: the card sitting, R-3a, the LLC navigator). New-front work (design, story kit, community) enters the same way — as briefs with censuses and returns, audited at full strength (capability never substitutes for verification).
2. **Parallel lanes, serial rulings.** Lanes run unattended and in parallel wherever their write-sets are disjoint (the five-lane afternoon of v56 b2 is the ceiling proof); decisions stay serialized through Nick in H10 form. Scale the number of lanes, never the number of decision-makers.
3. **The audit is the license to delegate.** The two-layer audit + census discipline is not overhead — it is the mechanism that makes a one-person company able to accept work from many agents. It scales by getting CHEAPER per lane (banked verdicts, pointer-form spines, the ~60 %-cheaper boot), not by being relaxed.
4. **Author ahead of need.** The hub keeps every next act pre-staged (the R-3b/R-4 pattern) so Nick's calendar, not the hub's throughput, is always the binding constraint — and idle hub beats go to the spine's own upkeep (W-SKILLS-5, rotations) that keeps future lanes cheap.
5. **New fronts open only at their gate.** Marketing opens at the register (post-R-4), identity at the word, community at R-10, sales at the fleet. A front opened before its gate produces un-backed words or un-audited work — both are debt, not progress.
