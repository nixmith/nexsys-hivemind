# The MHS datum — first read (2026-08-28)

## §1 What was announced (corroborated facts only)

Anthropic's **Model Hardware Standard (MHS)**: "a shared set of drivers and data formats that let systems communicate through one standardized layer" — devices "share data and accept instructions over a network without the need for a separate translator for each connection." It integrates with **MCP** so models "issue instructions through a shared control interface" and "receive structured feedback without custom glue code" — and, notably, devices supporting it "can be controlled directly through command-line prompts and API code files **even without a language model in the loop**." Launch surface: **laboratory and manufacturing instruments** (microscopes, liquid handlers, robotic arms; laser calibration on a quantum computer; Janelia Research Campus named), research preview with select labs/hardware makers in biotech/robotics/quantum, autonomous around-the-clock operation with "real-time fault detection." Anthropic "plans to build safety evaluations and develop best practices for AI systems that operate physical equipment" and to make MHS **open source** after the preview. **No consumer or smart-home application is mentioned in any coverage read.**

## §2 What it means for us (the first read, against the two strategy anchors)

1. **It is VALIDATION of the substrate bet before it is competition.** The largest frontier lab just declared that "the next stage of agentic AI may depend less on dramatic leaps in intelligence and more on the quieter work of making hardware understandable, addressable, and interoperable" — that is the Substrate Thesis's terrain, stated by the actor with the most to gain from pure model progress. The layer matters; the market now says so out loud.
2. **MHS is the CONNECTIVITY/TRANSLATION layer, not the ENFORCEMENT layer.** Drivers + data formats + MCP plumbing is, in home terms, the Matter-class problem — the layer that is being commoditized (first Matter, now MHS). Our thesis has never been "we connect devices"; it is **the harness enforces; the model only proposes** — event-sourced truth, measured `confirmed|unconfirmed`, explainability, the verdict corpus. MHS makes it EASIER for any agent to actuate hardware while its safety mechanisms are explicitly still-to-be-built ("plans to build safety evaluations") — which makes the missing deterministic floor MORE valuable, not less. D5's layered form holds exactly: MHS is plumbing for L2/L3 agency; L1 remains missing from the field, and the announcement itself concedes the gap.
3. **The posture is ADOPT-AND-SIT-ABOVE, never compete-with.** If agents standardize on MHS/MCP to reach devices, HomeSynapse should be REACHABLE through those standards as the home's enforcement gateway — agents talk TO the harness, never around it. Concretely: a future MHS/MCP-facing surface (the frozen read-API + a policy-gated command surface) is an INTEGRATION class beside Zigbee, not a rewrite. This compounds with model progress instead of being consumed by it — the exact bet.
4. **The standard it sets for US rises.** In a world where actuation is a commodity interface, the differentiator is trustworthy actuation: the claim register, the honest confirmation moat, the explain surface. Every public sentence and every roadmap row should be legible as "the layer that makes agentic hardware control safe in a home" — and nothing we ship should be positioned as connectivity.
5. **Trajectory/timing: no change to the Nov-25 runway.** Lab/manufacturing focus, research preview, open-source later — no consumer pressure this quarter. The watch-items: (a) the open-sourced spec, when it lands — does it grow interlocks/policy (the moat question); (b) any consumer/home partner in future waves; (c) whether MCP-side agent frameworks start assuming MHS-shaped device surfaces.

## ADDENDUM (2026-08-28, hub beat 9) — two corrections from the audited RS-3/W-MARKET-2 return; posture unchanged

The return (`context/research/2026-08-28_WMARKET2_market-currency_return.md` §M — audited ACCEPT; hub re-execution at the primaries 2026-08-28) corrects this file in two places:

1. **§2.2 "connectivity, not enforcement" is WRONG as written.** MHS *does* claim enforcement at the device envelope: *"MHS enforces device-level safety limits"* (Janelia, on Anthropic's page — hub re-verified verbatim 2026-08-28); *"device-declared bounds, interlocks, and emergency stops at the hardware interface, independent of the model"* (QuEra's own post); CMU: *"correctly blocked all six before any device moved."* That is an L0/L1 fragment at the DEVICE boundary — real, rooted outside the model, meeting the recognition rule for the single-device case. What remains unclaimed on every public surface: the cross-device policy kernel — permissions/principals, rate limits, reversibility classes, confirmation gates, cross-device invariants, attributed audit. Anthropic's own text defers that layer (*"physical safety roadmap"*; safety evaluations *"ahead of making the standard open source"*). D5 form of record: **the floor ABOVE the device is missing from MHS as announced, and MHS says so.** Building it is not "superior" to MHS; it is the part MHS leaves unbuilt.
2. **§2.1's "drivers and data formats… without a separate translator" wording was a secondary's (Developments Today), not Anthropic's.** The primary's own words: *"a standardized driver"*; primitives (*"read"*/*"write"*); natural-language *"tags"*; an auto-generated *"reference file… [including] what safety limits will be enforced"*; access via *"three such mechanisms: MCP, the command line interface, and code files (APIs)"* — MCP is one path of three; MHS is model-agnostic.

**Posture CONFIRMED, wording sharpened:** ADOPT-AND-SIT-ABOVE, where "above" = above the device envelope MHS enforces, at the cross-device/cross-principal composition point nothing in the field occupies (return §6.6-2). MHS-facing surface = INTEGRATION-CLASS-LATER; tripwires: the open-source release (undated) · "mhs" commits in LeRobot / strands-labs/robots · raspberrypi.com news · any OHF/HA MHS integration. The MCP-facing posture question is live NOW and correctly sits as the R-10 EXTERNAL-AGENT INTERFACE POSTURE row.
