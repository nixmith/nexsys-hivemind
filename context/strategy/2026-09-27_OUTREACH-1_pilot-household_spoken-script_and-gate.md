# OUTREACH-1 — the spoken script for one pilot household, and its gate

## §0 What this is, and is not
A script for Nick's own calls and conversations with people he knows — not a post, not a page, not a pitch to a stranger. Every sentence below is either LOGISTICS (no claim), a DESIGN FACT the docs of record state, or a MEASURED FACT a LIVE claim-register row holds (C-001 · C-002 · C-003). A sentence not in §2 is not said. The register is a source, not a surface: nothing here becomes a written or public text.

## §1 THE GATE (before every call; one glance)
1. **No name.** "It doesn't have a public name yet; the company is NEXSYS LLC." — the only answer to "what's it called". Never the candidate, never a hint, never the domain.
2. **No comparison, no "superior".** Nothing about what other products lack (the field-language preamble §0.1–.4 — the floor is MISSING from the field, and even that sentence stays in the register).
3. **No compliance, no privacy, no safety claim.** Not "compliant", not "secure", not "private by design", not "safe". Say what the software does; let the pilot see it.
4. **No sensing sentence.** Nothing about presence, occupancy or Wi-Fi sensing (the four-qualifier law).
5. **No number from research; only the two the record measured** (§2 rows 5–6), and only if asked.
6. **No promise of a date the record does not hold.** The pilot's date is "late November, if the 72-hour run at the end of October passes" — the run is the gate, said as a gate.
7. **Ask, then stop.** One ask per call (§2 row 9). A "no" is banked, never re-asked (arc 36).
