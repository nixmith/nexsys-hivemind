## §2 The open-core line (the hub's proposal; the household first)
**The household runs free and open — all of it.** The core (the event store, the projections, the automation engine, the integration runtime with Zigbee today, the REST/WebSocket read API, the dashboard with the explain surface and the recovery card, the installer `.deb`, the systemd units, the backup-and-restore of its own record) is Layer 1: complete, local, no account, no cloud, no per-device fee. The pilot household of §OUTREACH-2 runs exactly this, and nothing it runs is ever moved behind a tier (the revenue model's non-negotiable; the strategy of record §5).

**What a hosted tier adds — four things, each a convenience the house does not need to work:**
1. **The nightly off-device backup** — the desk already pulls BACKUP-1's closed copy over ssh (the `BACKUP-NIGHTLY:` lean); the hosted form is the same copy to a place the household does not run, restorable by the household's own key. The house keeps working with no backup at all; the tier sells the sleep.
2. **Sync across homes** — a second house (a parent's, a cabin) reads as one account's two records; each Pi still runs alone.
3. **The installer's fleet view** — for the person who installs for others (Phase 3's "Professional" row): every installed home's recovery record and nightly digest in one place, graded the way the bench grades the rig (A1's graders become the engine here — the one place the private corpus turns into a licensed product).
4. **The multi-site operator** — the property manager, the small facility: the accountability record across units; the north star's "Problem 1" customer. This is the layer the Substrate Thesis bets compounds with model progress and the one furthest from the pilot; it is named so the sequencing can say "not before".

**The line itself, in one sentence the register could someday hold:** *the house's record is the household's and runs without us; what we sell is keeping, joining and reading records for people who run more than one house or do not want to run a backup.* No fragment of the core moves across that line, ever — the test for every future feature is "does the house stop working without it"; a yes puts the feature in Layer 1.

**Two interlocks the line must respect before any of it ships:** (a) the LICENSE flip needs the OA §VIII(4) consent (the entity posture §3.2) — counsel's wave; (b) the community CHANNEL (Layer 2's hosted/verified content) needs the enforcing rung for third-party code (A-2: curated `IN_JVM` behind the quality gate; non-curated only at `RESERVED_SUBPROCESS`) — no "sandbox" word before that rung exists.
