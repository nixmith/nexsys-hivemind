# Brand-Architecture Decision Package (the G-2 input)

## 0. What resolves at G-2, and what this package is for

At the G-2 read (~Aug-5, on the Pelton comprehensive search + written analysis), Nick rules R-1: the product name. This package exists so that the SECOND ruling G-2 implies — *what structure the name drops into* — is already framed, priced, and recommended, and so the ruling takes minutes, not a session. Three architectures are on the table. All three are compatible with every G-2 outcome (GREEN / adverse-on-TAMORO / adverse-on-both); §5 maps the branches.

**Standing facts this package builds on (verbatim-class, not re-argued):**

- **ASIMTOTE is the locked company name** (2026-06-13; re-confirmed by the 2026-07-11 brief's screens). Its domain family (asimtote.com/.io + variants incl. asymtote.com) is owned. Its clearance question is packaged as a counsel companion brief (2026-07-15) and is **R4-decoupled** — nothing about TAMORO's timeline waits on it.
- **TAMORO is the leading product/ecosystem candidate** (the brand criterion of record, Nick 2026-07-22: tomorrow-evocation + catchy/brandable is load-bearing; a replacement must be BOTH clearable AND equally brandable). TAMODO is legal-fallback-of-last-resort (fails the evocation criterion). VEROMO is DEAD (declined-closed, v36 beat 4).
- **Editions extend by descriptor, never by new marks** (Core/Home/Pro/Enterprise — the branded-house rule, 2026-07-05 strategy §4, carried into the counsel one-pager). No architecture below reopens this.
- **Standard-characters word mark files first; the logo is a separate, later filing; logo spend is HELD post-G-2** (v36 beat 3 ruling). This package therefore prices word-mark programs only; the identity-system exploration (Deliverable 2) is paper-only until G-2.

## 1. The decision, stated in one line

**When the name resolves, does the consumer-facing brand run as (A) a two-register branded house — TAMORO forward, ASIMTOTE as parent — or (B) a single collapsed mark, or (C) a two-register house with the parent kept legally real but publicly near-invisible?**

## 2. The three architectures

### Architecture A — Two-register branded house, both marks public (the counsel-package's recorded intent)

TAMORO on everything a consumer touches (product, site, app, packaging, social). ASIMTOTE as the visible parent and the technical/corporate register — "Asimtote Core" as the shipping engine string, "a TAMORO product by Asimtote" class of lockups, both marks cleared and filed.

- **For:** matches the counsel package as written (the companion brief exists so ONE engagement quotes both). Preserves the asymptote-honesty story on the engine register, where the prosumer/developer audience actually reads it. Cleanest future separation of company reputation (B2B, licensing, investors) from consumer brand. If either mark is ever forced out, the other is a warm fallback with real equity.
- **Against:** two marks to clear, file, police, and foreign-file — roughly double the trademark program cost, forever (see §4). Two names for a stranger to absorb at launch, against a launch audience of finite attention. Splits the equity the launch window builds. The W-11-era lesson (the NexSys/HomeSynapse two-brand split was recorded as "a standing cost" that W-7 already half-collapsed) argues directly against re-creating a two-public-brand surface.

### Architecture B — Single collapsed mark (one name is the company AND the product)

Either TAMORO-only (company renames to Tamoro LLC/Inc; ASIMTOTE retires to a shelf) or ASIMTOTE-only (the 2026-07-11 brief's §0 REC — "Asimtote Core" ships, no second mark ever minted).

- **For:** the Google model the PM flagged in W-11 ("collapse to ONE brand"); one clearance, one filing program, one foreign wave, one name to build. Cheapest by construction. Maximum equity concentration.
- **Against (TAMORO-only variant):** discards a LOCKED company decision and an owned domain family for no consumer-visible gain; re-opens entity/chain-of-title work mid-engagement (the owner-at-filing/LLC question is already routed to the Pelton results consult); and concentrates everything on the one mark whose §2(d) landscape is precisely what we are paying Pelton to price — single point of failure at maximum exposure.
- **Against (ASIMTOTE-only variant):** fails the brand criterion of record. Nick's 2026-07-22 criterion makes tomorrow-evocation + catchy/brandable load-bearing for the CONSUMER mark; the naming record itself prices Asimtote's permanent radio-test tax and infrastructural register ("a warmth ceiling," 2026-07-11 brief §3.4). This variant was the right REC on 2026-07-11 and was superseded by exactly that criterion. Recorded as an option for completeness, not seriously in play unless G-2 goes adverse on every consumer candidate (§5, branch 3).

### Architecture C — Two-register house, parent quiet (REC)

TAMORO carries **every consumer-facing surface**. ASIMTOTE remains the real company — the legal owner, the entity name, the corporate/legal register ("© Asimtote LLC" · "a product of Asimtote" in the footer, W-7-style) and, optionally, the developer/engine register — but it gets **no consumer marketing surface, no lockup on packaging, no second logo program, and no independent brand-building spend**. One consumer mark is built; the parent is legally load-bearing and publicly whisper-quiet.

- **For:** keeps the locked company decision and its owned assets; keeps the chain-of-title/entity plan counsel already has; presents ONE name to strangers (the whole launch-attention budget lands on TAMORO); collapses the day-to-day brand program to one identity system while retaining the two-register legal structure the counsel package assumes. The Ubiquiti/UniFi analog the strategy record repeatedly cites is literally this shape: UniFi forward, Ubiquiti as the quiet parent.
- **Against:** ASIMTOTE's clearance/filing still costs real money for a mark consumers never see (though §4 shows the companion timing is deferrable per counsel, and the brief already allows "TAMORO leads, ASIMTOTE follows"). The engine-register question ("Asimtote Core" vs "TAMORO Core") must be ruled once, cleanly — see §6 Q2.

## 3. The Asimtote companion question, answered as a recommendation

The companion brief deliberately left timing to counsel ("on whatever timing counsel recommends; if budget forces a choice, TAMORO leads and ASIMTOTE follows"). This lane's REC sharpens that: **hold the ASIMTOTE filing decision to the Pelton RESULTS consult, alongside the owner-at-filing/LLC question that is already routed there** (v36 beat 5). Grounds: (1) the two questions are the same question — who owns the TAMORO filing determines how urgently the parent mark matters; (2) nothing public carries ASIMTOTE either, so the same no-public-use discipline that de-risks waiting on TAMORO de-risks waiting here; (3) the $2,500–4,500 Phase-1 guardrail is already carrying the TAMORO search — sequencing the companion behind the results keeps Phase-1 inside the rail. This is budget sequencing, not a legal judgment; counsel may advise otherwise and that advice governs.

## 4. Filings/cost mapping (banked benchmarks, pointed at source)

## 5. G-2 branch map (so the architecture survives any read)

1. **TAMORO reads clear-enough (counsel's call) →** Arch C executes immediately: R-1 ratifies TAMORO; §1(b) files standard-characters; the 48-hour launch-readiness checklist (Deliverable 4) runs; ASIMTOTE stays quiet parent; companion timing per the results consult.
2. **TAMORO reads adverse →** the ruled adverse-branch instrument runs (knockout-level screen + file on the fallback, ~$300–600 — v36 beat 4), with **TAMODO as legal-fallback-of-last-resort only**; the brand criterion forces the real question back to Nick: accept the evocation loss, or re-run the candidate funnel under the criterion. Architecture C itself is unchanged — it is name-agnostic. (This lane's Deliverable 2 marks exactly which identity assets survive that swap and what each costs to re-cut.)
3. **Everything consumer-side reads adverse (low-probability compound) →** Arch B-ASIMTOTE is the floor the program already owns: "Asimtote Core" ships under the locked parent with its owned domains, and the consumer-warm mark waits for a later, better-searched candidate. Nothing shipped pre-G-2 makes this branch expensive — that is what R-1-HELD is buying.

## 6. RULED (2026-07-23) — Architecture C RATIFIED; the engine register ruled

**Q1 — RULED: Architecture C RATIFIED** (Nick, 2026-07-23). TAMORO forward on every consumer surface; ASIMTOTE the real-but-quiet parent — legally load-bearing, publicly whisper-quiet, zero independent brand spend; companion filing timed at the results consult; editions by descriptor under the one consumer mark. Architectures A and B stand as documented G-2 contingency branches only (§5 governs their triggers). The grounds of record (as recommended and ratified): it is the only architecture that simultaneously (a) honors the brand criterion of record on the mark strangers meet, (b) preserves the locked company decision and the counsel package as built, (c) concentrates the launch window's attention on one name, and (d) prices the two-register legal structure at near-one-mark consumer cost — the one-pager's "parent/holding brand ASIMTOTE with TAMORO as the consumer ecosystem brand," executed with UniFi discipline.

**Q2 — RULED: "TAMORO Core"** (Nick, 2026-07-23). One public name on every surface incl. the engine register; a stranger never needs two names to discuss the product. The asymptote-honesty story lives in company boilerplate and engineering prose, not in a second product lockup. "Asimtote" appears publicly only as legal/corporate attribution (the W-7-successor footer class). Java namespaces stay deferred exactly as carried (Deliverable 4 row 6). Consequence for the token model: the product string pattern is "«{{productName}}» Core / Home / Pro …" — one token, descriptors free.

**What G-2 day now requires:** with Q1/Q2 ruled ahead of time, the only ruling left at the G-2 read is **R-1 itself** — the name, on the Pelton results. This package's remaining job is done; §5's branch map governs contingencies.
