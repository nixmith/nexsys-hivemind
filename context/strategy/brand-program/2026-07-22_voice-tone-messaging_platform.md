# Voice, Tone + Messaging Platform (name-agnostic — the name is a token)

## 1. The trust-brand story (the platform's spine)

**The one-sentence brand:** *{{productName}} never lies about your home.*

Every smart-home platform tells you what it *sent*. {{productName}} tells you what actually *happened* — and can prove it. When it says a lock locked, the lock reported back. When it can't know, it says so, calmly, with the reason. When an automation fires — or doesn't — you can ask why and get a plain-language answer that is never deleted. The system runs entirely in your home, works with the internet unplugged, and keeps a complete, replayable record of everything it did and why.

**The hero, always phrased as the three questions** (the consumer surface of the moat):

1. *Why did it fire?*
2. *Why didn't it fire?*
3. *Did it actually happen?*

**Message hierarchy (ruled order — lead with the durable halves):**

1. **The honest outcome** — `Confirmed / Sent, not confirmed / Failed` on every action; never a false "Confirmed." (Lead. The trust win competitors' optimistic state doesn't ship.)
2. **Never-evicted answers** — explanations are permanent projections of a complete event log; the run you need is always reconstructable. (Co-lead; the structural half.)
3. **"Why didn't it fire?"** — the three-way honest distinction (never triggered / condition false / device didn't confirm). (Prominent co-equal, not the sole lead.)
4. **Local-first, no cloud account** — the *frame* that makes the honesty credible, not the differentiator (competitors are local too).
5. **Reliability / works-together** — the mass-market lead per D-4; **privacy** is the prosumer/EU segment expression, never THE lead.

**The tomorrow register (the brand criterion's story, name-agnostic):** the forward-looking note always attaches to **the home, never to AI** — "your home's tomorrow," "the home that's ready for tomorrow," "built to still be right years from now." AI capability is described as gradual, advisory-first, under-the-hood, residential. No verbal or visual echo of any tomorrow-adjacent AI branding (deliberate distance, per the counsel record).

## 2. One voice, three registers (inherited, restated for the new-mark era)

Identity: **infrastructure-grade clarity delivered with quiet human respect** — precise, calm, confident, never hype.

- **Register A — Senior Engineer** (docs, API, changelogs): declarative, assumes competence, explains why.
- **Register B — Calm Neighbor** (homepage, onboarding, blog): unhurried, context before detail, warm without casual; never urgency or sales pressure.
- **Register C — Direct Neutral** (UI microcopy): no self-reference (no product name, no "we"); what happened / what to do; never blames, never cheerful, never apologetic.

The stranger ("mom") test is the acceptance bar for anything explanatory, in every register.

## 4. Boilerplate paragraph (name-agnostic; drop-in at G-2)

> **{{productName}}** is a smart-home platform that never lies about your home. It runs entirely on hardware in your house — no cloud account, no monthly fee, working even with the internet unplugged — and it treats honesty as an engineering guarantee: an action is reported as confirmed only when the device itself confirms it happened. Every automation can answer "why did it fire?", "why didn't it?", and "did it actually happen?" in plain language, from a complete record that's never trimmed or deleted. Built for people who want a home that simply works — and proof that it did. {{productName}} is a product of {{companyName}}.

## 5. Elevator pitch (30 seconds, name-agnostic)

> Every smart-home platform has the same dirty secret: when it says your lock is locked, it usually means it *sent the command* — not that the lock actually locked. {{productName}} is the platform built around not lying to you. It confirms what devices actually did, tells you honestly when it can't know, and keeps a permanent, plain-language answer to "why did that happen?" — or "why didn't it?" It runs entirely in your home, no cloud required, on hardware you own. We think trust is the entire product — so we engineered it instead of marketing it.

(The last sentence is Register B warmth with a claims-clean spine; "we think" keeps it opinion, not superiority claim.)

## 6. The claim rails (binding on every downstream consumer of this platform)

**Say freely (true, defensible, in-product provable):** never reports confirmed unless the device confirmed · distinguishes never-triggered / condition-false / didn't-confirm · answers are permanent projections of a complete log · works with the internet unplugged · no cloud account required · honest "unknown" instead of a silent blank.

**Never say (pre-counsel or corrected-baseline):** "the only / unique / first / patented" (use "to our knowledge" framings, sparingly) · "we invented command confirmation" (claim the durable explainable *outcome*, not the mechanic) · "competitors' traces vanish on restart" (HA persists traces to disk; the accurate limits are the trace cap and the absent never-triggered trace) · any patent-family attribution without re-verification · "plug-and-play" (W-4 embargo until the install story is ruled) · anything a future paid/cloud tier would have to retract (Connect-proof rule).

**Competitive posture:** state our behavior, not their failure, wherever possible; where comparison is necessary use the corrected baselines above and re-verify currency at ship time (the HA watch is a standing item).
