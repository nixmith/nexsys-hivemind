4. **DOMAIN + NAMESPACE POSTURE.** Exact `.com` free or marketplace-buyable at a sane, pre-set walk number (per-name walk set at shortlist time; the tamoro.com doctrine generalizes); handles/GitHub/npm obtainable. 5. **MULTI-MARKET LINGUISTICS.** No negative, comic, or profane readings in EN · JP · DE/NL · FR · ES/IT/PT; clean katakana; pronounceable everywhere. **Run at DESK time, not post-search** — the VERDOMO exhibit below (a thesis-perfect name killed in one minute by Dutch) is why this test runs early and cheap.
6. **LONGEVITY.** Survives category expansion (energy, eldercare, robotics) and the ten-year test; no feature-era coupling; **no dwelling-type narrowing** (a name that says "apartment" or "house" excludes the other — the EvoLoft/LOFT analysis below is the exhibit).

## 2. The domotics-root doctrine (Nick's question, answered precisely)

**What "domotics" is:** the EU/LatAm category word for home automation — *domotique* (FR), *domótica* (ES/PT), *domotica* (IT/NL) — from Latin *domus* (home) + *-ique/informatics*. In English it is a niche technical term; US consumers do not know it.

**The asymmetry that makes it good brand material:** in the **US**, a DOMO element reads as a warm coined syllable pair (no category meaning → arbitrary-leaning, strong distinctiveness); in the **EU/LatAm**, the same element reads suggestive-of-category (instant comprehension: "something home-automation") — you get meaning where the category word exists and distinctiveness where it doesn't. That is a genuinely rare property; most roots give you one or the other.

**The three rules that make a domo-play VALID (and their reasons):**
- **Subordination:** the domo element must be SUBORDINATE — `X + domo`, where X dominates the impression. Never the bare category word, and avoid `DOMO + X` forms (leading position is dominant position: a DOMO-first mark maximizes both the Domo Inc. containment issue in 9/42 and the EU descriptiveness question).
- **Warmth:** the X must carry consumer warmth (zen, nest, ever) — the doctrine exists to serve a consumer trust brand, not to smuggle a category label into a logo.
- **Named-neighbor awareness:** any domo-play inherits three standing screen items — Domo Inc. (US 9/42 BI software, active portfolio), the EU domotics-crowded field (Domotz, Domoticz, Domadoo, Indigo Domotics…), and NHK's DOMO licensing in Japan. None screened as a blocker for ZENDOMO; all three ride every domo-candidate's counsel brief by name.

**Costs, stated honestly:** EU exclusivity will be somewhat narrower for any domo-formative (crowded suggestive field — coexistence is the norm there); and the family's `.com` inventory is largely swept by brand marketplaces (§3's screen results: three of four domo-candidates sit on Atom/Squadhelp/BrandBucket). Neither is disqualifying; both are priced.
