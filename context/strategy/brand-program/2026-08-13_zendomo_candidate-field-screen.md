## 3. The comparative read (the direct answer)

**The field we left:** TAMORO's post-search field was counsel-priced **moderate-to-high** — a near-homophone senior (TOMORO) with shared "tomorrow" impression, actively prosecuted (the deadline-day Examiner's Amendment), OpenAI-majority-owned, inside a diluted TOMORROW-formative field, PLUS a surname refusal risk stacking on top, PLUS three more register conflicts (TASORO Cl 11 · TAMOP Cl 9 smart-home wording · ALTAMORO ×2 fresh Cl 11 ITUs).

**ZENDOMO's field, free-layer:** **no near-twin, no shared-commercial-impression senior, no single adversary of TOMORO's class, no surname corpus.** The structural exposure is element-based (contains DOMO; leads with ZEN-), which is a different and materially lighter shape: element conflicts argue about dominance and impression against a crowded backdrop; near-twin conflicts argue about the whole mark against one motivated senior. Rough sizing: **the worst named neighbor (DOMO Inc.) prices low-moderate against TAMORO's priced moderate-high** — comparable to TAMODO's field-lightness, WITH the evocation TAMODO lacked (TAMODO was legal-fallback-of-last-resort precisely because it meant nothing).

**The honest counterweights:** (1) this is a desk screen — the TAMORO lesson is exactly that the paid search finds what the free layer cannot (phonetic variants: SENDOMO/XENDOMO/ZENDOMA-class; state registers; design marks); (2) the EU suggestiveness trade (narrower exclusivity where domotics is generic); (3) two well-resourced element-owners (Domo Inc., Zendesk-as-posture) exist even if neither is a likely blocker; (4) zendomo.com is aftermarket, price unknown until asked (never disclose the project to the marketplace — the standing broker discipline).

## 4. Brand-criterion + system fit (the strategy half)

**The criterion of record (2026-07-22)** made *tomorrow-evocation + catchy/brandable* load-bearing. ZENDOMO deliberately swaps the evocation axis: **calm/home ("Zen Domotics") instead of futurity ("tomorrow")**. The hub's analysis — the axis swap is a strategic UPGRADE, not a loss:

1. **The tomorrow-axis is the collision class.** The evocation that made TAMORO attractive is what placed it beside TOMORO and the whole TOMORROW-formative diluted field. A second tomorrow-formative candidate re-enters the same field on purpose. The calm/home axis vacates it entirely.
2. **Calm is the recorded brand substance.** "Consumer-grade calm" is a named value in the 2026-07-05 candidate record (Calmkor: "names your 'consumer-grade calm' value outright"); the voice platform's identity is "infrastructure-grade clarity delivered with quiet human respect — never hype"; the product story is the home that quietly works and never lies. Futurity was always the more hype-adjacent, AI-echoing register — the exact echo the counsel record instructs us to avoid.
3. **System fit is near-lossless.** 3 syllables, 7 letters, terminal -O, clean radio test (zen-DOH-moh), unambiguous spelling; carries TAMORO's double-O visual signature ("the two apertures") into the W-B wordmark, which re-renders from the token in an afternoon; "ZENDOMO Core / Home / Pro / Enterprise" scans cleanly under the branded-house rule; Architecture C is name-agnostic by construction. The D-A dawn-arc device exploration survives with its reading shifted calm-ward (its secondary read — a signal rising above a threshold, the confirmation moment — never depended on "tomorrow" at all). Only the initial-bound D-C app-icon direction (T-based) dies; it was paper.
4. **Multi-market linguistics, free-layer:** JP — zen is Japanese-origin (positive), "dōmo" echoes a friendly greeting/thanks and a beloved mascot (no negative meaning; NHK's marks are the JP-screen item); EU/LatAm — zen + domo(tics) is suggestive-positive; Esperanto "domo" = house (a literal "zen house"); IT/ES "duomo/domo" house/dome-adjacent. No profanity or negative-connotation flags surfaced in the majors. Native-speaker verification rides the paid program as before.
