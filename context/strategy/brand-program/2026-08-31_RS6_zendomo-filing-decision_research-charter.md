## §1 The questions — falsifiers first
1. **The enforcement-propensity read (THE crux for the shutdown axis).** Characterize Zendo, Inc. (myzendo.com; Reg. 8398495) at primaries: company size/origin/funding; app traction in the US (both stores: ratings count, last-update date, rank if visible); commercial trajectory (active/growing/dormant). THEN the enforcement history: TTAB proceedings by/against them (USPTO TTABVUE search); any C&D/dispute footprint (news, forums, GitHub, app-store complaint chatter); their counsel-of-record on the registration. A dormant two-person studio with zero enforcement history and a live-but-small app is a different risk than a funded, growing, enforcement-active operation. FALSIFIER: an enforcement-active or fast-growing US Zendo = the filing lean likely dies.
2. **The registration record itself.** Pull Reg. 8398495 at TSDR: filing/registration dates, the identifications VERBATIM, basis, any disclaimers, the specimen. Same for the Deep AI ZENDO (6961839) and the MAZIGH DOMO (pending US application + the WIPO designation status — is the US designation examined/refused/pending?). Dates matter: a Sept-2025 first-use claim means shallow common-law seniority depth.
3. **The crowd arithmetic.** Count LIVE ZEN-formative marks in cl. 9/42 and LIVE -DOMO-formative marks in cl. 9/11/42 at the USPTO public search (floors acceptable, disclose truncation). The crowd is the coexistence argument's ammunition — quantify it instead of asserting it.
4. **The shutdown-vector ranking.** For each mechanism, what it costs THEM and what it does to US: (a) TTAB opposition (registration-only; typical cost/duration); (b) federal infringement suit (the true stop-use vector; cost floor; small-entity appetite evidence); (c) **platform complaint routes — the CHEAP vector** (Google Play / Apple App Store trademark-complaint processes; what they can and cannot reach for a local-first OS whose product is not primarily app-store-distributed; the future companion-app exposure); (d) UDRP/domain. Evidence-based ranking, not theory.
5. **Mitigations, priced by precedent.** ID-narrowing (cl. 9+42 drawn to local-first home-automation OS/device-control, away from design/layout language); consent/coexistence agreements with small senior users (how common, typical shape); house-mark pairing (NexSys ZENDOMO) — does the case law/practice literature say it materially helps?; §1(b) timeline arithmetic (months to first office action vs our G-2/launch calendar — does the examiner's answer arrive BEFORE brand investment regardless?).
6. **VERDOMO sanity screen (knockout-grade only, NOT a comprehensive search).** USPTO public search + obvious web/domain/app collisions for VERDOMO: is the backup OBVIOUSLY cleaner, obviously dirtier, or unknown? (The -DOMO crowd applies to it equally — say so.) This prices the SWITCH branch honestly.
