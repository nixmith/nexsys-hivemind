# RS-8 — the successor-name coinage sprint (the inverted screen)

## §0 What the program has learned, in five lines (do not re-litigate)
1. Three names died on ONE mechanism: a sound/string neighbour already operating in the connected-home channel (ASARUM's Zendo on HA/Homey · Whisker on HA · the -DOMO software crowd). The channel test was never run first. Now it runs first.
2. Counsel's frame is first-to-USE: a foreign micro-company with USD pricing and US downloads is "in our market". A name's worst neighbour is graded on size, trajectory, enforcement history, and whether it is in the connected-home channel — not on whether it has filed.
3. The spoken form is the name. A coinage that a listener writes as another brand's word (WISCOR → whisker) is dead before law. The dictation test is a kill gate, not a taste row.
4. The comprehensive search is the LAST instrument, fed ONE name that survived every free screen. Nick's cap covers one.
5. The criterion v2 governs generation: evocation on-thesis (calm · home/shelter · honesty/trust · permanence); excluded axes futurity/AI and tech-generic; 2–4 syllables (3 ideal); works bare and as "«name» Core/Home"; no surname corpus; no descriptiveness collapse in any target market; no dwelling-type narrowing.
