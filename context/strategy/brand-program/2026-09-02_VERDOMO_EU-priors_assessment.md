## §2 The analysis — three axes, each graded

### §2.1 The US rights axis (the filing we are about to make) — UNAFFECTED
Neither record is a US right. A foreign application or registration confers no US rights absent US use; the US is first-to-use, and neither party uses the sign in US commerce. The §44(d) priority window from the Spanish filing (six months from 9 Dec 2025 → 9 Jun 2026) has **closed**; a §44(e) US filing by Mr Arendt would need the Spanish registration to issue, a bona fide intent to use in US commerce, and would cover **class 36 housing-rental services** — not 9/42. VERDOMONDO's owner has no US filing on the record (RS-8: verdom*/verdome* in the US = 4, all dead). **The US examiner will see neither.** The US question remains the one already on counsel's desk: the standalone-DOMO family in 9/42 (the expected, recoverable citation).

### §2.2 The EU axis (a later phase; the strategy's EU filing is not this month's act) — LOW, on the recoverable axis
- **(A) the Spanish trade name.** A registered *nombre comercial* is a relative-ground earlier right in Spain and can be invoked against later Spanish marks — and, if used in trade of more than mere local significance, against an EUTM under Art. 8(4) EUTMR. But every such ground requires **similarity of goods/services**: class 36 housing management and apartment-rental listings versus a home-automation operating system (cl. 9) and its SaaS (cl. 42) are **dissimilar** on any ordinary reading — different nature, purpose, channels and consumers; a villa keyholder is not a software vendor. A sole trader on Mallorca with a local business is also the definition of "mere local significance" for Art. 8(4). Grade: **very low.** The one practical residue: he owns the .es and the Spanish search page today; a US software brand launching in the EU later would out-rank a local firm quickly, or coexist — no confusion in fact is plausible.
- **(B) VERDOMONDO (EUTM, cl. 38).** The sign is the closer problem: VERDOMONDO contains VERDOMO whole — sight high (7 of 10 letters, identical onset), sound medium-high (ver-do-MON-do / ver-DO-mo: the first two syllables identical, stress differs), meaning divergent (green + world vs true/green + home). **EUIPO does not examine relative grounds ex officio** — an earlier EUTM bites only if its owner OPPOSES within three months of our EU publication. Services: "provision of access to an electronic marketplace [portal]" (cl. 38) versus home-automation software/SaaS (9/42) — at most a **low degree of similarity** in EUIPO practice (access-provision vs software; different purpose and public); under the interdependence principle a high sign-similarity can offset low service-similarity, so an opposition is **arguable, not strong**. Propensity: an individual owner, a Danish-language filing, an expired .com, no visible business — but a recordal filed in March 2026 says the registration is being kept alive. Grade: **low-to-moderate, EU-only, and manageable** by (i) drawing the EU specification tightly to home-automation software (nothing marketplace-shaped), (ii) a consent/coexistence approach if she opposes, or (iii) narrowing on opposition — all on the recoverable axis. **Counsel's row for the EU plan; not a reason to withhold the US filing.**
