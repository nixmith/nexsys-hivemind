### §1.2 The sentence that changes the calculus (and the hub's own miss)
Counsel's frame is **first-to-USE, not first-to-file.** US priority flows from use in commerce; a §1(b) filing date beats any LATER APPLICATION of theirs (Nick's stance, true) but does NOT beat their EARLIER USE (the stance's unpriced half). ASARUM's US use (~Apr–May 2026; USD pricing on the site; US store presence; US downloads) predates any filing of ours — so a ZENDOMO filing makes NexSys the senior APPLICANT and the junior USER at once, and a later ASARUM application (or an opposition/cancellation resting on prior use, within five years) argues from the stronger seat. Multi-currency pricing is the "in our market" fact counsel weighted: it is use directed AT US consumers, not incidental spill-over. **The hub's RS-6 audit weighted enforcement PROPENSITY (tiny UK micro-consultancy; no appetite; no war chest) — a correct read of the SHUTDOWN axis that does not answer the RIGHTS axis counsel graded.** The two-axis frame (v59 close mint) was the right frame; the hub applied one axis.
