## §1 The deliberation — where the lanes agree, where they part, how the hub rules
**Agreed by both lanes and confirmed by the hub at seven primaries:** WISCOR has zero exact records live anywhere outside one Chinese WISCORE (RAKwireless, cl. 9); Whisker holds five live cl-9 marks plus WHISKEROS at the Statement-of-Use stage; WHISKER CONNECT's identification is literally IoT device-control software; the Whisker app carries 144K iOS ratings and shipped yesterday; "Whisker" is an official Home Assistant integration under its own name; Automated Pet Care Products filed six TTAB oppositions in 2025 with Crowell & Moring; ~83% of US/Canadian speakers merge wine/whine; wiscor.com is a $2,699 broker listing glossed "Wisconsin + corporation". ZINDOMO is empty everywhere and keeps the Z_NDO string counsel weighted.

**Where they part:** RS-7 says WISCOR's one problem is a single, well-formed DuPont question that a paid search answers — send it. The second opinion says the question's answer is predictable and the brand defect precedes the legal one — don't.

**The hub's ruling, on three grounds:**
1. **The spoken-form collision is a brand defect no opinion cures.** For a home product that will be named aloud — to a partner, on a podcast, to a voice assistant, into a search box — the most common spoken form of WISCOR is /ˈwɪskɚ/, which IS the adversary's mark. Every word-of-mouth mention routes to a litter-box brand with 144K ratings and a search-engine "did you mean". Even a written opinion of "distinct goods" leaves that tax in place for the life of the brand. RS-7's own rubric scores spell-from-sound 2/5 for exactly this reason and then leans on the name anyway — the rubric was right.
2. **The legal prior is adverse, and the instrument that just graded ZENDOMO tells us how.** Counsel retired ZENDOMO over a 500-download UK micro-company because it was in the market and could grow. Whisker meets every axis the RS-6 charter named as the lean-killer profile — funded, growing, enforcement-active — at roughly three orders of magnitude, with a product literally called WhiskerOS ("operating systems … for automated pet care devices and appliances") and an integration in the very ecosystem we run in. The pet-care limitation is real, and it is one adjective standing between "IoT device-control software" and "home-automation OS" under marks an examiner will hear as the same word. RS-7's "every opposition targeted the literal string" is absence of a test case, not evidence of restraint — WISCOR would be the test case. The hub does not need counsel to price that; it needs counsel for names whose question is open.
3. **It fails Nick's own utility function.** Secure it, stick to it, stop thinking about it. A name that needs a written opinion to survive its own pronunciation is not that name. What RS-7 got exactly right is the PROFILE to want more of: a coined whole word, nothing on any register, an exact semantic fit. WISCOR proves the profile exists; the sprint's job is to find one without a homophone in the channel.

**On ZINDOMO:** counsel's oral warmth was an unresearched first reaction; the record says the vowel buys a cheaper refusal-response, not a clean file, and deletes the only meaning the name had. Retired without further spend.

**On VERDOMO:** RS-7 scores its positioning fit 2/35-row; the hub notes for the RS-8 rescorer that ver- (verus, verify, veritas) + domo carries "the verifiable home" — closer to "why did it fire?" than a 2 suggests — against the 08-13 finding F-N1 (Romance ears read VER- as verde/vert, green, not vero) — and says no more; taste is scored by a lane on the rubric, not felt by the hub. Its legal question is the DOMO crowd (67 live in 9/11/42; DOMOTZ in 9/42 network-monitoring is the nearest software neighbour; MAJORDOMO pending; DOMOS ×3 pending) — coexistence-friendly on the register precedents RS-6 banked, and loud.

## §2 The mint — THE CHANNEL-NEIGHBOUR-FIRST SCREEN (folds to pm-lessons at the close)

The screening order was inverted — linguistics and taste first, the channel last. **The law:** for every candidate, in this order, each stage a kill gate: (S1) the string AND its homophones/near-spellings against the connected-home channel — the Home Assistant integrations index, Homey/SmartThings/Hubitat catalogues, Matter/CSA product names, Alexa/Google partner names, both app stores; (S2) the live USPTO search — exact, phonetic, variant spellings, cl. 9/42 (+11/35/38 screen) — and TMview for §44 claimants; (S3) no crowded morpheme (ZEN · DOMO · CORE · SMART · HOME · NEST · HUB · SENSE · SYNC · IQ · AI); (S4) the spoken-form test — say it three ways; what does a listener type; what does a search engine correct it to; (S5) domain at RDAP (never "available" from a chat claim) + handles; (S6) linguistics; (S7) the taste rubric.
