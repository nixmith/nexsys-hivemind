# The attorney-search draft — the inquiry email

## §1 Whom (Nick's pick; the same email to each; three to five)
Louisiana-licensed business attorneys or small firms whose practice lists small-business / startup formation and governance AND software or technology IP (an assignment, a license change) — not a trademark boutique (trademark counsel is already engaged for the application), not a litigation practice. Prefer a firm that says it works with single-member LLCs and fixed-fee document packages. Send the same email to each; the first reply that answers all four questions in §3 with a fee is the one to engage (an engagement by Oct 15; the two documents by Nov 2).

## §2 The email (paste whole; two slots: `[Firm / Attorney name]` and your phone)

**Subject:** Louisiana single-member LLC — an in-place name change, a member consent and an IP assignment (engagement sought by mid-October)

Dear [Firm / Attorney name],

I am the sole member of NexSys LLC, a Louisiana limited liability company formed in July 2025, and I am looking for business counsel for three bounded matters ahead of a software release this November. The company builds home-automation software; the product's name is held under a pending trademark application handled by separate trademark counsel, and the product name is not yet public, so I refer to it here only as "the product."

The three matters:

1. **An in-place name change of the LLC.** At the product's first public step (an app-store listing, a public install flow or first sale, or the first investor conversation), I intend to rename the LLC to the product's name — the same entity, the same EIN, never a new company. The question I need answered, and the one I would like to be the first thing we settle: *confirm that an in-place name change of the Louisiana LLC (same EIN) preserves the intent-to-use trademark application filed in the LLC's current name, and the recording steps at the USPTO.* I would also want the Louisiana filings for the name change itself handled or checked.

2. **A member consent under the operating agreement.** Before the release, the company will change the software's license to Apache-2.0. My operating agreement requires a written member consent for that decision (its §VIII(4)); I need the consent drafted so that it can be executed on your word, in the same window as the name change.

3. **A confirmatory intellectual-property assignment.** All of the software was written by me; I need a confirmatory assignment from me to the LLC, drafted so that it is clean for the license change above and for any later investor diligence.

What I am asking of you:

- Whether these three matters are within your practice, and whether you have handled an in-place LLC name change alongside a pending trademark application before.
- Your fee structure for them — a fixed fee for the document package if you offer one, or an hourly estimate with a cap.
- Your availability: I would like to engage counsel by October 15 and have items 2 and 3 in signable form by November 2.
- A conflicts check against NexSys LLC and me personally.

I keep a written record of decisions and can send the operating agreement, the formation documents and the trademark application's details on request. A short call works if that is easier than email.

Thank you for your time.

Nick Smith
Sole member, NexSys LLC
[phone]

## §3 What the reply must carry (the gate for the engagement)
All four: (1) yes to the three matters, with item 1's question answered in principle or named as the first deliverable; (2) a fee (fixed or capped); (3) the dates held (Oct 15; Nov 2); (4) no conflict. Anything else is a follow-up question, not a no.
