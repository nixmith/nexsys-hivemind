# §2 The one question to read on sight, before the search begins

PALOKI contains three live Class 9/42 strings whole — **PALO**, **LOKI** and **OKI** — and I would rather you see them now than on day four. Our own pass says none of them is blocking; that is exactly the kind of conclusion I want a lawyer's signature on, not ours.

**(a) OKI — the one to price.** OKI Electric Industry Co., Ltd. (Japan) holds OKI in Class 9 (Regs. 1578683, 3840953, 6542585, 4593286 — telecom switching, modems, peripherals, printers). It is the one owner in this neighborhood with a record of acting against letter-plus-OKI marks: it opposed OKIUSA (Opp. 91177751, 2007) and filed an extension of time against IOKI (Serial 77768585, 2009) — nothing since 2009. Against that: 879 live Class 9/42 marks contain "oki," the goods are remote from home-automation software, and OKI sits at the unstressed end of our word. One fact in OKI's favor that we measured ourselves: in an automated speech test the only systematic mishearing of PALOKI was to "OKI" (8 of 36 trials). **The ask:** is a watch notice or opposition from OKI Electric plausible, and would a §2(d) claim be sustainable given the crowding? If you think it is blocking on sight, tell me before starting the search and we will redirect the same engagement, at the same fee, to a fallback the same day.

**(b) LOKI — the wording our identifications avoid.** Raintank, Inc. (Grafana Labs) holds LOKI in Class 9 (Reg. 6949788, software for logs and machine-generated data) and Class 42 (Reg. 6542537, *"providing on-line non-downloadable software for multi-tenant log aggregation, monitoring, and analysis"*). Our Class 42 identification excludes that wording by name, and both classes exclude log aggregation and machine-generated-data analytics for enterprise IT. The record we found: 33 live Class 9 marks contain "loki"; six of nine LOKI-formative Class 9 marks we pulled registered with no office action; and **TALLOKI** (Serial 98598376 — a two-syllable prefix plus LOKI, our exact shape, filed after Grafana's Class 9 registration issued) drew a non-final action on January 2, 2025 that says verbatim: *"The trademark examining attorney has searched the USPTO database of registered and pending marks and has found no conflicting marks that would bar registration under Trademark Act Section 2(d)."* One caveat we would ask you to carry: TALLOKI's goods (appointment-scheduling software) are remote from Grafana's, so that precedent shows the Office not treating LOKI as the dominant element of a prefix-plus-LOKI software mark — it does not show the Office clearing such a mark on goods near Grafana's. That is why the exclusion in A2 is load-bearing rather than cosmetic. Grafana's only TTAB appearance is as a defendant (92091183). **The ask:** does the exclusion do its job, and does our own "monitoring" language (home-automation devices and scenes) stay far enough from theirs?

**(c) PALO — low, please confirm.** Jedox AG holds PALO in Class 9 (Reg. 3828440 — spreadsheet and OLAP software for financial planning); David Pearson holds PALO in Class 42 (Reg. 5550984 — design services). Palo Alto Networks has 37 live marks and six TTAB proceedings, every one over CORTEX and none over the PALO string. The words sound different (PALOKI /pælˈoʊki/ against PALO /ˈpɑːloʊ/), PALO sits under our unstressed first syllable, and the goods are remote.
