## FQ-1 — Beachhead order + discovery authorization — **RULED: Option A**

**Ruling (Nick, 2026-07-24):** STR/vacation-rental operators → MDU property managers → regulated verticals explicitly deferred to expansion, ratified as the *discovery hypothesis* (not the final beachhead). The ~20-interview discovery program is AUTHORIZED: ~12 STR operators, ~4 MDU managers, ~4 integrators/installers, mom-test discipline, pre-written scripts, no pitching. **The final beachhead ruling is a Phase-0 exit criterion, made on interview evidence.**

**Why (recorded):** STR pain maps 1:1 onto what is already silicon-proven — "the guest claims the lock never worked; here is the provable event history" is the never-false-CONFIRMED verdict + custody fence + honest availability, with the acceptance-arc positioning notes providing today's honest claim bounds. Decision-maker = checkbook; weeks-long cycles = fast learning. Rejected: ruling STR final without discovery (repeats the intuition-compounding error class at segment level); MDU-first (worse first-contract shape for a solo founder: longer cycles, procurement friction).

**Consequences:** the beachhead-discovery lane charter is dispatch-ready (`2026-07-24_lane-charter_beachhead-discovery.md`); it is also the FQ-3 trial vehicle. R-1 discipline binds: interviews run under a neutral research posture with no candidate mark; the "public use" definition for any borderline act rides the already-approved Pelton results-consult agenda item.
