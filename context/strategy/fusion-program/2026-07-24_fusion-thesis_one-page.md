# The Fusion Thesis — Attestable Privacy (one page)

**The endgame in one sentence:** local AI + the provenance layer = **attestable privacy** — not *"we promise your data never left"* but *"here is the tamper-evident, independently verifiable record proving how your home ran — and what never left it."*

**The stack (each layer already real or ruled):** an immutable, causally-chained event log as the single substrate (built; CI-enforced; "local-first, cloud, and AI are the same log read differently" — Doc 17) → honest outcomes as first-class data (never-false-CONFIRMED verdicts, silicon-certified with measured bounds) → the provenance layer (hash-chain + Merkle proofs + COSE signatures over exported segments — profiled from RFC 9162/COSE/SCITT, never invented; FQ-2) → selective disclosure (prove a property — "all sensors operational, no leak events" — without revealing raw data; the Assure pattern) → local AI as a structural proposer over the same log (AIOT-INV-1: AI never actuates; the deterministic engine disposes; every AI decision is a pure, auditable projection) → the Phase-3 appliance, where platform attestation (RATS-shaped, RFC 9334) binds "this record came from this attested runtime on this device."

**The science, stated honestly (the claim's real bounds):** cryptography proves *integrity* and *inclusion* — that the record is complete-as-committed, untampered, and signed by a known key. Proving the *negative* ("no data left") additionally requires attesting the software that ran: measured boot + platform attestation of the runtime, plus verifiable egress accounting. v0 therefore claims exactly what a log can prove (tamper-evident history; provable attestations derived from it); the *attested-runtime* strengthening is the Phase-3 research arc — named as future, never claimed early. What no architecture can prove: physical-sensor spoofing and insiders-with-root; the spec's threat model says so out loud. This honesty IS the moat's credibility — a trust company that over-claims attestation once has no product left.

**Why incumbents structurally cannot follow:** Big Tech's economics require the data to leave — attestable privacy is a promise their business model cannot sign (counter-positioning, front 1). HA has the values but not the substrate — no immutable causal log, no confirmation ledger, and an installed base that makes re-founding unschedulable (architectural-debt counter-positioning, front 2). The fusion is only claimable by an architecture that committed to event-sourcing, local-first, and honest outcomes at its foundation — which is what we did.

**The trickle-down mechanism (Nick's framing, made explicit):** commercial deployments are the proving ground — STR/MDU operators pay for audit artifacts, multi-site reliability, and fleet operations, which battle-hardens exactly the properties (honest verdicts, custody, availability honesty, provenance) that then flow DOWN to single-family users as free-tier reliability and privacy, and UP as enterprise trust. The single-family user never pays for this hardening and is never its product; they inherit it — the four-layer model's no-extraction covenant, kept.

**Where the EE-research/patent arc plugs in (without touching the FOSS covenant):** the attestation boundary between silicon and log — secure-element key custody, measured-boot binding of log segments to runtime state, efficient attestation on constrained/NPU-class hardware — is separable hardware/attestation IP that can be patented and licensed WITHOUT encumbering the Apache-2.0 core or its users (the core's patent grant stays clean; that separability is a design requirement, and its exact line is a standing counsel question — not-a-lawyer). Grad school compounds here instead of competing (FQ-6: SBIR/STTR-class funding fits this arc natively).

**Phase mapping:** Phase 0–1 ships the log's honesty and the v0 provenance profile; Phase 2 gets it consumed by institutions; Phase 3 fuses it with local AI and attestation in the appliance. Every step advisory-before-autonomous (AIOT-INV-1).

**Falsifiers (what would disprove this thesis — watched, not feared):** discovery shows commercial buyers won't pay for audit artifacts (FQ-1 evidence) · a credible provenance standard emerges from elsewhere first (watch tripwire T6) · HA ships a durable confirmation ledger + tamper-evident log without a rewrite (T2/T3) · attestation-on-constrained-hardware proves impractical at appliance cost (the EE arc's own null result). Each has a named watcher; none is currently firing.
