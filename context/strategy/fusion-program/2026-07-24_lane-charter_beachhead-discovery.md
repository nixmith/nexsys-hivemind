# Lane Charter — Beachhead Discovery (the interview program + the co-founder trial)

## 1. Mission

Produce the **evidence that rules the beachhead**. The deliberation record ranked STR-first on structural argument; this lane replaces argument with ~20 recorded conversations. Its output is a synthesis memo that CONFIRMS or FALSIFIES: (a) STR operators feel the audit/reliability pain concretely and recently; (b) they (not us) describe artifacts they'd pay for; (c) the FQ-5 five-feature priority order as seen from the segment; (d) how buying actually happens (who signs, what cycle, what channel). Secondary output: the integrator-channel map. Tertiary (structural): the FQ-3 trial evidence.

## 2. The discipline (mom-test, stated as rules)

1. **Talk about their life, never our idea.** Past behavior and specific incidents only: "walk me through the last time a guest disputed something about the unit" — never "would you use a system that…".
2. **No pitching, no product, no mark.** The posture: independent research into operations/audit/reliability pain in short-term-rental and multi-dwelling management. Zero candidate-mark use (R-1).
3. **Bad news is the goal.** A falsified hypothesis at interview #6 is a cheaper lesson than at deployment #1. The synthesis memo must contain a "what we were wrong about" section — an empty one is a red flag, not a win.
4. **Concrete beats general.** Every claim in the synthesis cites interview #, quote-class evidence, and whether it was volunteered or prompted. (The house VERIFIED/UNVERIFIED discipline, applied to humans.)
5. **Commitment and advancement are the currency:** an operator who offers their time again, an intro, or "call me when you have it" is signal; compliments are not.

## 3. Segments, counts, sourcing

| Segment | Count | Who qualifies | Sourcing channels (the lane verifies current ones) |
|---|---|---|---|
| STR operators | ~12 | Manages ≥5 units, remote or multi-site; uses smart locks/sensors today | Operator communities/forums, property-manager meetups, local STR associations, warm intros; integrator referrals |
| MDU property managers | ~4 | Portfolio ≥50 units; any smart-access/IoT deployment live or planned | Property-management associations, vendor-neutral events, warm intros |
| Integrators/installers | ~4 | Installs/manages smart-access or building-IoT for the above segments | Installer directories, distributor communities, the two segments' own vendors |

Interview channel: video or phone, 25–40 min. Recording only with explicit consent; otherwise contemporaneous notes. No compensation beyond gratitude (keeps signal clean); a follow-up-results offer is fine.

## 4. Script skeletons (starting points — the lane's first act is to finalize them)

**STR operators:** last guest-dispute incident (lock/access/damage/noise) → what evidence existed, what was missing, what it cost (time, refund, review, insurance) → current stack (PMS, locks, sensors, monitoring) and what breaks → what happens during an internet outage at a unit → who they'd call to fix any of this and what they pay today → (only if volunteered) what a "provable event history" would need to show to matter.
**MDU managers:** last access-audit or liability incident → compliance/reporting obligations they actually file → current building systems + the vendor relationships → procurement reality (who approves, what cycle, insurance requirements) → staffing around incidents.
**Integrators:** what they install and why · what fails and eats margin · what their customers ask for that nothing provides · how they'd evaluate a new platform (the channel-requirements question) · what certification/training they expect.

**Hypothesis ledger (maintained from day 1):** H1 audit-evidence pain is top-3 for STR operators · H2 the checkbook is the operator (no committee) · H3 outage-resilience is felt money · H4 audit-export + fleet view outrank org-hierarchy/RBAC for STR (FQ-5 re-cut input) · H5 integrators are the scalable channel · H6 MDU cycle-length makes it second, not first. Each interview marks every hypothesis supported/undermined/silent.

## 5. The trial structure (FQ-3, operationalized)

Week 0: scripts finalized; conversion criteria signed by both (the FQ-3 list verbatim); calendar blocked. Weeks 1–2: Nick joins interviews 1–5 (calibration; contact protection). Weeks 3–8: the friend runs solo, batch reviews after every 5 (notes quality, discipline drift, hypothesis-ledger currency). Deliverable: ≥15 of ~20 interviews + per-interview notes + the synthesis memo. Off-ramp: poor batch-1 quality → pre-agreed conversion to integrator-channel desk research, no drama. No equity, no title during trial; post-trial anything is a separate counsel-involved negotiation.

## 6. Deliverables + done-when

1. `discovery/interview-notes/` — one file per interview (dated, anonymized IDs; PII kept out of the repo).
2. `discovery/hypothesis-ledger.md` — living; every interview updates it.
3. `discovery/synthesis-memo.md` — the beachhead-ruling package: hypothesis verdicts with evidence density, the wrong-about section, segment priority REC, FQ-5 re-cut REC, channel map, and the go-forward interview/pilot pipeline (who said "call me").
4. Trial assessment (Nick-only conversation, not a repo file beyond a one-line disposition).

**Done-when:** the synthesis memo supports a one-turn beachhead ruling by Nick (Phase-0 exit criterion) and the FQ-5 re-cut checkpoint has its input. Target: synthesis by ~mid-October 2026.

## 7. Escalation + rails

STOP-and-escalate to Nick: any request to demo/sell (we're not selling; park the contact for Phase 1) · any press/publicity interest (publish gate governs) · any act that could read as public mark use · any interviewee requesting NDA/paperwork (counsel's lane). The gate window is untouched by construction — this lane never touches core/bench/FE. Honest-claim: if asked "does this exist?", the true answer today is "the core platform exists and is being certified; the commercial features are being designed from conversations like this one."
