# Fusion Strategy — Phase Plan of Record (2026-07-24)

**The doctrine the plan expresses (convergence, consumed):** the moat lives in the layers around an Apache-2.0-forever core; both fronts are counter-positioning plays (business-model lock vs Big Tech; architectural debt vs HA); the log is the platform and products are projections; expand from strength, never the reverse; every hardware move is fabless and traction-gated; the endgame is attestable privacy (the fusion thesis, same tree).

**The dependency spine (order is law):**
Gate window (freeze 08-14 EOD → read 08-16) → G-2 (Pelton results ~Aug-5 + the two G-2-day acts) → the W-2/W-5 publish gate → flag-plant + community start (the brand program is the instrument) → Phase-0 exit on discovery evidence → Phase-1 feature work (post-gate V1.x only) → traction gates → hardware moves → Phase-3's big bet. AX-7 precedes any AI-authoring ship (Doc 17 §7-4). Counsel gates every uniqueness claim, every publication-vs-patent call, every entity/instrument act.

## Phase 1 — Launch → validated beachhead traction: win the one beachhead

**Entry:** Phase-0 exit complete — most load-bearing: the beachhead ruled on discovery evidence, MVP launched, publish gate open.

**The work:**
1. **The beachhead motion.** First paying pilots in the ruled segment, priced as real contracts (FQ-6: revenue is funding source #1). The integrator channel is seeded from the discovery program's integrator interviews. Sales collateral inherits the honest-claim rails (positioning notes + the FQ-5 floor).
2. **The FQ-5 commercial set, as V1.x projections.** Design brief (post-gate lane, already chartered) → discovery re-cut checkpoint → formal AMDs where named (ScopeRef materialization; Doc 09 auth) → V1.x milestones the hub sequences on the standing cadence. Each feature ships → its claim unlocks (present tense only after the artifact exists).
3. **Ride Matter.** The winter Matter arc per the integration roadmap REC (§0: Matter-over-WiFi/Ethernet first, Thread on a dedicated radio as phase 2; CSA membership/certification decision at arc start). Never replicate HA's integration library; breadth stays data-first (D5).
4. **Provenance v0 publishes** (FQ-2 trigger: publish gate open + demand confirmed) — spec + reference implementation + the standalone verifier; the signed-audit-export feature is its product twin. First counterparty conversation opens (Assure-seed insurer class), formalization deferred until one engages.
5. **Reference appliance (hardware move #1) — traction-gated, not launch-gated.** Entry condition: paying beachhead deployments demonstrating repeat demand + a hardware-margin model that stands on its own (the locked Layer-4 $149-class golden path). Fabless; predictable-hardware-target lesson (HA Green class); never a $30 commodity plug.
6. **Community + ecosystem.** The FOSS community program runs (post-publish-gate); Doc 18's seam activations (SDK surface honesty: "the adapter contract is frozen and documented") proceed on the strategy's 0–12-month promise WITH the governance footprint Doc 18 already reserved.

**Exit criteria:** N paying commercial deployments in the ruled beachhead segment (N set at Phase-1 entry from discovery evidence — deliberately not invented here) · at least one external party consuming provenance v0 (verifier used in anger) · the FQ-5 set shipped or visibly shipping (claims unlocked feature-by-feature) · integrator channel live (first certified/partnered installers) · reference appliance shipped or consciously deferred by ruling · competitive watch green (no unanswered tripwire).

**Do-NOT-do in Phase 1:** no enterprise/up-market expansion (finish the beachhead first) · no second beachhead segment · no regulated-vertical entry (nursing homes/clinics stay expansion-deferred) · no edge-AI hardware · no standards-body formalization without a counterparty · no equity conversations unless the FQ-6 trigger fires · no per-device fees, no paywalled community content (DP-18-C), ever · no autonomous-AI features of any kind (AIOT-INV-1; AX-7 still gates AI-authoring).

## Phase 2 — Traction → moat: compound the position

**Entry:** Phase-1 exit complete; the beachhead is won on evidence (repeat purchases, reference customers, integrator pull).

**The work:**
1. **Cloud/hybrid services as revenue + EARNED switching costs** — the Nabu-Casa analog on the locked four-layer model; Connect/Cloud Pro class services, additive-only (Doc 17 §3.4: cloud strictly additive; no key ever leaves the machine). Switching costs are earned (data gravity, integrations, certified installers) — never punitive.
2. **The standard into compliance/insurance use.** The engaged counterparty (Assure-seed) consumes attestations in production; formalization proceeds (SCITT-profile shape, permissive license, us as reference implementation); the Trust-Mark/certification seed (locked Phase-4 stream) begins earning its ladder.
3. **Ecosystem growth.** Integrator program scales; the Doc 18 marketplace floor activates when the months-12–24 window and evidence justify it (per-version scanning, kill-switch, two-tier labeling — the floor is already ruled).
4. **CSA seat** + standards posture (the trifecta: thought-leadership + open standard + best implementation).
5. **Expansion sequencing begins from strength:** up (light-enterprise on the proven commercial set) and down (prosumer benefits from commercial hardening) — order per evidence.
6. **Funding:** revenue + grants carry; the patient-equity contingency exists only behind its named trigger (FQ-6).

**Exit criteria:** recurring commercial revenue covering the commercial arm's run-rate · the standard consumed by ≥1 institution as evidence-of-record · certified-installer base measurable · switching-cost surfaces live and earned · the competitive position vs HA/Big Tech re-assessed quarterly with no unanswered tripwire.

**Do-NOT-do in Phase 2:** no license change, ever (the moat is never allowed to depend on the code) · no hostage-taking switching costs (the anti-Oracle line) · no data monetization regardless of scale · no edge-AI appliance until Phase-3 entry conditions · no chasing Big Tech's mass-market channel on their terms (counter-position, don't out-bundle).

## Phase 3 — Scale → the big bet: the edge-AI appliance and attestable privacy

**Entry (all required):** Phase-2 exit complete · sustained revenue base · the provenance layer institutionally consumed · team capacity beyond solo-founder (the commercial team exists) · the EE-research arc producing at the attestation boundary.

**The work:**
1. **The edge-AI appliance** — NPU-equipped, local voice/vision/automation intelligence, zero cloud round-trip. The moat is the FUSION, not the NPU (NPUs commoditize): local AI + the provenance layer = **attestable privacy** (the thesis one-pager, same tree, carries the honest form of the claim and the science behind it). Fabless, capital-light throughout.
2. **AIOT-INV-1 governs every step** — AI proposes, the deterministic engine disposes; advisory-before-autonomous is the law of the whole arc; every AI decision auditable as a pure projection of the log (Doc 17 §3.3). The appliance's framing inherits this: intelligence that is structurally a proposer.
3. **The EE-research/patent arc** feeds the attestation boundary between silicon and log — the patentable, licensable layer that lives OUTSIDE the Apache-2.0 core covenant (hardware/attestation IP, never the FOSS core). Counsel owns FTO/claims strategy; the FQ-2 publication checkpoint discipline continues.
4. **Expand up and down from strength** — enterprise on the proven institutional surfaces; single-family users inherit the battle-tested reliability, audit, and privacy properties from commercial hardening (Nick's trickle-down framing — the thesis §4 mechanism).
5. **Drive the standard others build to.**

**Exit:** open-ended — this phase IS the destination state compounding.

**Do-NOT-do in Phase 3:** never let the appliance's AI cross AIOT-INV-1 · never patent-encumber the FOSS core or its users (Apache-2.0's patent grant stays clean; OEM patent-safety is WHY the license was chosen) · never claim "provably private" beyond what the attestation actually proves (the thesis's honest-claim section is binding copy law).

## Standing program-wide rules

1. **Gate sovereignty** — through 2026-08-16, this program charters nothing that touches core/bench/FE gate lanes.
2. **Claims track artifacts** — the FQ-5 floor + the acceptance-arc anti-claims list govern every external sentence; counsel gates every "only/unique/safest."
3. **Every lane is write-isolated** and returns to the hub for two-layer audit; Nick commits explicit-paths; no lane orders commits.
4. **Quarterly re-read:** the competitive-watch return triggers a phase-plan currency check (tripwire-fired items may re-sequence, never silently).
5. **The focus rule:** one beachhead at a time; declining a segment today costs almost nothing tomorrow because products are projections (the log is the platform — the doctrine that makes focus survivable).
